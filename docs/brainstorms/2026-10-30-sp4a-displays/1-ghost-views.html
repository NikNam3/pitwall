1 · Rejoin ghost: crossing order and gap chart (V03, V04)

Sample race for every page: Silverstone, L23/52. Our cars KUH (P6, medium, 23 laps old) and LIN (P9, stopped L15). The ghost is where KUH would be if it boxed at the end of this lap, per condition (rejoin.car). Grey by default; colour only to tell the three conditions apart. Our cars are white. Gap sign as SP3: a car ahead is negative, behind positive. Hover texts are in the captions (a screenshot cannot hover).

The ghost marker (kit piece, spec 8.3): one style in all three displays

 ◇KUH  green     ◇KUH  VSC       ◇KUH  SC
in a table: ◇ before our code, the row in the condition colour
in a chart: dotted line in the condition colour, ◇ at its newest point
on the map: a hollow ◇ in the condition colour; our car is the white dot
Condition colours are the theme's muted series roles: green condition = series3, VSC = series5, SC = series2. They only distinguish; they are not the flag colours, so they never read as a flag or an alarm. The ghost line is dotted because the teammate's line is already dashed.
