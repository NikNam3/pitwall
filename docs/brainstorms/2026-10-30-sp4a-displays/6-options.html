New Q1 · the rejoin ghost

Rules for every variant: the ghost is quieter than the real cars (our white car stays the brightest thing on every display, the rivals keep their normal grey or team colour), and no glyph sits inside the driver code: the ghost row reads plain KUH. Each variant is one style for the crossing order, the gap chart and the track map together.

the condition hues (series3, series5, series2) at 55 % opacity: the row, the dotted line and a hollow ring in the hue, dimmer than any rival; the condition word in its hue.

New Q9 · the undercut highlight

"Our undercut is possible" = a positive margin on an AHEAD row; its mirror "their undercut on us is possible" = a positive margin on a BEHIND row (amber, advisory). Both must be stronger than white bold. The theme has no neutral "good" hue: its roles are text greys, ownCar white, critical red, warning amber, best purple, personalBest green, the flag colours and the muted series.

A · lit cell (recommended)

lit cell: a positive margin is an inverted cell, dark text on the light text colour (ours) or on amber (theirs). No new hue; the strongest mark that stays inside the colour rule.
