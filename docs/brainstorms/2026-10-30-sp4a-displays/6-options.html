New Q1 · B, and how it marks the slot

B stays the ghost's look: faint, our code plain, no glyph; the gap chart's faint lines (G dotted, VSC dashed, SC dash-dot, the letter at the end) and the map's faint rings are the same in every variant. The variants differ only in the crossing order: how the ghost row shows where it slots in between two cars, with solid 1 px hairlines in the faint role, no dashes and no leader dots. Every row keeps the exact columns of the real rows.

New Q9 · B (colour): the hue for "our undercut is possible"

A positive margin on an AHEAD row is bold in a new theme role, proposed name opportunity; on a BEHIND row it stays bold amber (warning). The hue must not clash with purple (session best), green (personal best), amber, red or the blue flag. The theme's series roles are muted (oklch 0.72, chroma 0.09) and read weaker than white bold, so the candidates are full-strength values except series6 for comparison.
