## Decisions in these mockups

- **SP3 carries over:** `L23` is a lap number, counts say `laps`; fixed column grids with right-aligned numbers; gap sign ahead negative; white bold = relevant now; amber = advisory; red = critical; compound as a grey letter; our cars white (teammate dashed in charts).
- **Ghost (kit piece), B chosen (new Q1):** one style for the crossing order, the gap chart and the map, always quieter than the real cars, our code plain: a faint row (`textFaint`), faint lines told apart by dash and a letter (G, VSC, SC), faint rings with their letter on the map. How the row marks its slot is open; pages 1 and 2 show the recommended B1 (solid faint hairlines above and below).
- **Crossing order:** INT is `intervalMs`, the gap to the car ahead in the projected order; P is the order item's `position` (Q4 A).
- **Source badge (kit piece):** one cell after a prior-based value: none = measured this session, `ˢ` = stored, `ᵈ` = default; every such value carries the hover (`measured · 6 stops`, `stored · 3 stops`, `default · community value, Silverstone (R3)`). Tables that have room show the source in words (pit-loss table, stint compounds). (Q2 A; the cards read the new `lossSource`, Q5 A)
- **Numbers only:** no card says box, undercut works or switch now. The undercut shows a positive margin in bold colour (new Q9 B): the proposed `opportunity` role for our undercut (hue open, page 3 shows cyan), bold amber for theirs. The pit sweep marks `over` in amber. The SC card under SC/VSC brightens the active row.
- **Unknown values** are empty cells with a dotted underline and the reason on hover (kit `unknownReason`); `notApplicable` cells are blank with the hover but no underline.
- **Realism:** no field of the 4a displays is hidden for our own cars; a rival's hidden wear only changes the basis inside the analyses. Positions stay on the map. Each page says so where it applies.
- **Track map:** rivals as dots in their team colour (Q7 B) with faint codes, our cars white and larger; turn labels outside each bend, car codes inside the circuit, labels never overlap each other or the line and stay inside the panel; in-pit cars hollow; incident markers amber triangles at the topic's `point`, labelled with the incident kind (Q8 B); a yellow marshal zone redraws its stretch of the outline in `flagYellow`; a car under blue gets a `flagBlue` ring (new Q16); the white tick is the start line, grey ticks the sector starts.
- **Gap chart:** no end labels for cars, as the existing widget; the ghost lines span the last 6 laps (Q6 A) and the reference line in the header names their losses.
- **Pit sweep:** FINISH is `finishDeltaMs` against the fastest row (`+0.9`, Q10 A); the selector's options come from `pitWindow.car` (Q11 A).
- **SC card:** one line in green running (`if SC P9 gain +10.5ᵈ · VSC P10 gain +7.2ˢ`), the sheet under SC/VSC (spec 8.2).
- **Lap overview:** heat = background brightness within the stint (dark = best pace lap, light = worst; a ramp from `bgBand` to `textFaint`); laps outside the pace set dim, no heat; `i`/`o` marks; VSC/SC as a yellow strip under the row.
- **Workbooks (approved as a starting point, Q15 A):** `strategy` puts every call on main (tower, crossing order, map, gap chart, undercut, SC card, pit sweep); the side gets the forecast plus the `race` workbook's car-care set. `longrun`: our pace on main, the knowledge it feeds (pit loss, crossover) on the side.

## Kit and theme additions these mockups assume (Task 7)

- `formatLapTimeTenths` (`1:30.4`, charts and the lap grid), `formatOffsetMin` (`now`, `+30`). The pit sweep's delta uses the existing `formatSignedSeconds`.
- The ghost (style per new Q1: colour role, dash patterns per condition, letters, the map marker) and the source badge (glyphs, hover text) as kit pieces; a theme `heat` ramp for the lap grid; a new theme role `opportunity` (new Q9; recommended `#3fd8e8` in every variant: `quiet`, `quiet-roomy`, `quiet-compound-colour`, `quiet-personal-best-unlit`); the blue-flag ring uses the existing `flagBlue` (`#5ca8ff`), the yellow zone the existing `flagYellow` (`#ffd23f`).
- Noticed, not in scope: kit `cellBox` counts a cell as 0.6 em (8.4 px at 14 px); JetBrains Mono's advance is 8.0 px (measured in headless Chromium), so the app's cell counts are about 5 % conservative. The mockups draw at the real 8.0 px; the layouts page uses the app's 228 × 53.

## Contract additions for the flags (new Q16)

`trackPositions.cars` (state, 10 Hz) gains:
- `zones: { startM: Metres; endM: Metres; flag: ZoneFlag }[]`: every marshal zone in lap order, from `SessionState.marshalZones` (`startFraction` × the track length; a zone ends where the next one starts, the last one at the line). The display draws only `yellow`; `green`, `none` and `unknown` stay grey.
- per car `flag: Known<FiaFlag>`: the car's `fiaFlag`. `unknown` from the game becomes `unknown('notReceived')`. The display marks `blue`; a per-car `yellow` is already visible as its zone and gets no mark of its own.

The game sends a single yellow (`ZoneFlag` is `unknown | none | green | blue | yellow`), so the map has no double yellow.

## Questions of round 1 (answered)

**Q1. Ghost marker colours.** The mockups show A.
- A: `◇` plus muted series roles per condition (green `series3`, VSC `series5`, SC `series2`), dotted lines.
- B: one colour for all ghosts (white, like our car) and the condition as a word only.
- C: flag roles (green `flagGreen`, VSC and SC `flagYellow`); VSC and SC then differ only by their word.

Recommendation: A. The colour only tells three things apart, and B makes the map's three ghosts look the same. C makes the ghosts read as flags or alarms.
**Answer:** none of A/B/C (user, 2026-10-30): the ghost was brighter than the real cars and `◇` inside the code looked odd. Replaced by **new Q1** above; see page 6.

**Q2. Source badge glyphs.** The mockups show A.
- A: superscript `ˢ` stored, `ᵈ` default, no mark when measured this session; hover on all.
- B: a mark on every value, `ᵐ` included.
- C: no visible mark, hover only.

Recommendation: A. The normal case stays clean, and a guessed value is still visible at a glance.
**Answer:** A (user, 2026-10-30).

**Q3. Compound letters.** The contracts carry `ActualCompound` (C1–C5) in `stintAnalysis.car` (stints, `CompoundFit`), `lapOverview.car`, `pitSweep.car` params and `pitWindow.car`. The mockups show S/M/H letters (SP3 A1), which need the visual compound.
- A: add `visual: VisualCompound` next to each compound in those value types.
- B: the displays join our car's tyre sets (`car.detail`) to map actual to visual.
- C: show C-numbers.

Recommendation: A. B fails while the sets are `notReceived`, and C breaks SP3's A1.
**Answer:** A (user, 2026-10-30).

**Q4. Crossing-order rows.** `rejoin.car` `order` items have no position, and `gapToNextMs` does not say its direction. The mockups show a P column and INT as the gap to the car ahead in the order.
- A: add `position: number` to each order item, and define `gapToNextMs` as the gap to the car ahead (or rename it `intervalMs`).
- B: the display numbers the rows from the ghost's `position` (arithmetic in the display).
- C: drop the P column.

Recommendation: A.
**Answer:** A, with the rename `gapToNextMs` → `intervalMs` (user, 2026-10-30).
