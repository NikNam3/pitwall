## Decisions in these mockups

- **SP3 carries over:** `L23` is a lap number, counts say `laps`; fixed column grids with right-aligned numbers; gap sign ahead negative; white bold = relevant now; amber = advisory; red = critical; compound as a grey letter; our cars white (teammate dashed in charts).
- **Ghost marker (kit piece):** `◇` in the condition's colour, the same in the crossing order (before our code), the gap chart (dotted line, `◇` at its newest point) and the map (hollow `◇`). Condition colours: green = `series3`, VSC = `series5`, SC = `series2` (muted, they distinguish; not the flag colours). Dotted, because the teammate's line is already dashed. (Q1)
- **Source badge (kit piece):** one cell after a prior-based value: none = measured this session, `ˢ` = stored, `ᵈ` = default; every such value carries the hover (`measured · 6 stops`, `stored · 3 stops`, `default · community value, Silverstone (R3)`). Tables that have room show the source in words (pit-loss table, stint compounds). (Q2)
- **Numbers only:** no card says box, undercut works or switch now. The undercut highlights a positive margin: white bold on an AHEAD row (our undercut), amber on a BEHIND row (theirs). The pit sweep marks `over` in amber. The SC card under SC/VSC brightens the active row.
- **Unknown values** are empty cells with a dotted underline and the reason on hover (kit `unknownReason`); `notApplicable` cells are blank with the hover but no underline.
- **Realism:** no field of the 4a displays is hidden for our own cars; a rival's hidden wear only changes the basis inside the analyses. Positions stay on the map. Each page says so where it applies.
- **Track map:** rivals grey dots with faint codes, our cars white; turn labels outside each bend, car codes inside the circuit, labels never overlap each other or the line; in-pit cars hollow; incident markers amber triangles with their label; the white tick is the start line, grey ticks the sector starts.
- **Gap chart:** no end labels, as the existing widget; the reference line in the header names the ghosts with their losses.
- **SC card:** one line in green running (`if SC P9 gain +10.5ᵈ · VSC P10 gain +7.2ˢ`), the sheet under SC/VSC (spec 8.2).
- **Lap overview:** heat = background brightness within the stint (dark = best pace lap, light = worst; a ramp from `bgBand` to `textFaint`); laps outside the pace set dim, no heat; `i`/`o` marks; VSC/SC as a yellow strip under the row.
- **Workbooks:** `strategy` puts every call on main (tower, crossing order, radio window, map, gap chart, undercut, SC card, pit sweep); the side gets the forecast plus the `race` workbook's car-care set as a proposal (Q15). `longrun`: our pace on main, the knowledge it feeds (pit loss, crossover) on the side.

## Kit and theme additions these mockups assume (Task 7)

- `formatLapTimeTenths` (`1:30.4`, charts and the lap grid), `formatRaceTime` (`1:18:44.6`, pit sweep), `formatOffsetMin` (`now`, `+30`).
- The ghost marker (glyph, the three condition roles, the dotted dash) and the source badge (glyphs, hover text) as kit pieces; a theme `heat` ramp for the lap grid.
- Noticed, not in scope: kit `cellBox` counts a cell as 0.6 em (8.4 px at 14 px); JetBrains Mono's advance is 8.0 px (measured in headless Chromium), so the app's cell counts are about 5 % conservative. The mockups draw at the real 8.0 px; the layouts page uses the app's 228 × 53.

## Open questions

**Q1. Ghost marker colours.** The mockups show A.
- A: `◇` plus muted series roles per condition (green `series3`, VSC `series5`, SC `series2`), dotted lines.
- B: one colour for all ghosts (white, like our car) and the condition as a word only.
- C: flag roles (green `flagGreen`, VSC and SC `flagYellow`); VSC and SC then differ only by their word.

Recommendation: A. The colour only tells three things apart, and B makes the map's three ghosts look the same. C makes the ghosts read as flags or alarms.
**Answer:**

**Q2. Source badge glyphs.** The mockups show A.
- A: superscript `ˢ` stored, `ᵈ` default, no mark when measured this session; hover on all.
- B: a mark on every value, `ᵐ` included.
- C: no visible mark, hover only.

Recommendation: A. The normal case stays clean, and a guessed value is still visible at a glance.
**Answer:**

**Q3. Compound letters.** The contracts carry `ActualCompound` (C1–C5) in `stintAnalysis.car` (stints, `CompoundFit`), `lapOverview.car`, `pitSweep.car` params and `pitWindow.car`. The mockups show S/M/H letters (SP3 A1), which need the visual compound.
- A: add `visual: VisualCompound` next to each compound in those value types.
- B: the displays join our car's tyre sets (`car.detail`) to map actual to visual.
- C: show C-numbers.

Recommendation: A. B fails while the sets are `notReceived`, and C breaks SP3's A1.
**Answer:**

**Q4. Crossing-order rows.** `rejoin.car` `order` items have no position, and `gapToNextMs` does not say its direction. The mockups show a P column and INT as the gap to the car ahead in the order.
- A: add `position: number` to each order item, and define `gapToNextMs` as the gap to the car ahead (or rename it `intervalMs`).
- B: the display numbers the rows from the ghost's `position` (arithmetic in the display).
- C: drop the P column.

Recommendation: A.
**Answer:**

**Q5. Source of the losses on the cards.** `rejoin.car` `conditions[]` and `scCall.car` `options[]` carry `lossMs` without its `KnowledgeSource`, so the crossing-order titles and the SC card cannot show the badge that spec 8.1 asks for. The mockups show the badge.
- A: add `lossSource: KnowledgeSource` per condition and option.
- B: no badge on the cards; the pit-loss table carries the source.

Recommendation: A. The SC card's gain rests on the SC default at a new track, and that is exactly when the engineer needs to know.
**Answer:**

**Q6. Ghost lines: span and sign.** Spec 7.5 says gapper plus loss; plan Task 26 says "minus the loss". In the gapper's sign (positive = behind the reference) the ghost is our line plus the loss. The mockups draw plus, over the last 6 laps.
- A: plus the loss, over the last N laps (`ghostLaps` config, default 6).
- B: plus the loss, over the whole history.

Recommendation: A. Over the whole history the ghost lines cross every other line and say nothing. Fix the plan's wording either way.
**Answer:**

- B: team colours.

**Q8. **Q9. Undercut card, BEHIND rows.** For a car behind, the mockups read `deltaMs[n]` as what the rival gains if it stops now and we cover n laps later, and `marginMs` as that gain minus the absolute gap. A positive margin is amber (their undercut on us would work); on an AHEAD row it is white bold.
- A: as shown, with the contract stating that the margin uses the absolute gap.
- B: the same highlight (white bold) on both sides.

Recommendation: A. A threat and a chance should not look the same.
**Answer:**
