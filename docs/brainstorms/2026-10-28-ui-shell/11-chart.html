11 · Chart and colours

Chart lines use muted colours of equal brightness, assigned per chart so neighbours differ most. Our driver's line is only thicker, not brighter. PIA is dashed and faint: its data is stale. The SC phase is a lighter grey band. Axes and labels are the same font and size as the tables; a wider panel shows more laps, not bigger text.
