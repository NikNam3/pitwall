3 · Gap chart (V04)

The gapper (race history chart): each car's gap to a virtual car running the reference pace, per lap. Flat = on reference pace, a line that drops = losing time to it, rising = gaining. ALB's step on lap 22 is its time loss. Our car is the only white line (thicker); every other car has its team colour, teammates dashed (UI shell rule). The SC phase is a band; the field bunches behind it. The reference is in the header and can be switched to another car's average or a fixed lap time (a topic param). Axes are always labelled; a wider panel shows more laps, never thicker lines.
