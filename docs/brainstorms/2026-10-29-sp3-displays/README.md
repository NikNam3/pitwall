## Decisions

- **A1: compound as a letter only**, grey. The `quiet` theme's compound roles stay text grey; `quiet-compound-colour` stays available as a variant.
- **B2: purple (session best) for every car, green (personal best) only for our cars.** `sectorStatusRole(status, { ours })` implements it (plan Task 7).
- **White bold = relevant in the current race moment.** No new hue: the moment brightens values, it doesn't colour them (ERS in the battle moment, the battle row in DRS range). Amber = out of band or advisory, red = out for a full lap, critical, negative margin, as before. The amber left bar in the tower marks a car in a major incident during the incident moment.
- **Lap number vs lap count:** `L23` always means a lap number; a count always says it: `+1.8 laps`, `17.6 laps`, `1.62 kg/lap`, `M · 12 laps old`. In a table column the unit sits in the header (`%/LAP`, `LAPS TO 70%`), and the values are plain numbers. Formatters: `formatLapNumber(n)` → `L23`, `formatLaps(n)` → `1.8 laps`.
- **Column grids and number alignment:** every multi-column display is a grid with fixed column widths. Numbers are right-aligned in a fixed field with fixed decimals per value kind, so `.` and `:` line up. A value that doesn't fit a column's format goes elsewhere instead of breaking the column (the projected lap's FINISH margin is in the Δ column as `38.2 s`, not under the lap times).
- **Tyre life:** `LAPS TO 70%` = laps until the corner's wear reaches the limit at the current rate. In the bar, `|` = the limit, `:` = the game's life span.
- **Fuel:** labelled rows `MARGIN` (= LASTS − TO GO), `LASTS` (laps the fuel lasts at this burn, with kg), `TO GO` (laps still to race), `BURN`, `SAVE` (empty unless the margin is negative), `GAME` (dim, the game's figure).
- **ERS:** no descriptor row (the attack level is the `|` in the bar; its value on hover and in settings). The STORE row shows the live flow, `▼ deploying`, `▲ harvesting` or nothing when idle (from the MGU-K power). `DEPLOY 2.1 MJ this lap, limit 4.0` (the per-lap limit, not the battery), `HARVEST`, `TREND` with "attack ready", "attack in N laps", or nothing when falling below the level. The deploy mode is not shown.
- **Battle cards:** one row per neighbour with GAP, CLOSING (s/lap), TO DRS (laps or "in DRS"), PACE Δ (positive = we are faster), MARGIN (pace Δ minus the 1.0 s overtaking threshold), TYRES (ours then theirs), ERS (empty when hidden by realism).
- **Projected lap:** grid TIME / POS / Δ; Δ is "to best" in the PROJ row and "margin" in the CUT-OFF row; FINISH = time left on the clock at the line.
- **Main screen = timing and race awareness, side screen = car care** in `quali` and `race`.
