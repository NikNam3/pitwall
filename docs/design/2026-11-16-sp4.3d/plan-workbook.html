Planning round · after qualifying


Between qualifying and the race (spec §7): the boards read the planning job (source: planning), the race inputs on their first line, A and B prefilled with accept. The strategy table below each board shows the planning job's full ranking (the same rows the board's DP part is drawn from, the same plans.preview topic), so it is useful in the planning round too. Field plans shows notApplicable: no race runs, and the planning job publishes no field forecast (a follow-up if wanted: the rivals' predicted start compounds and first stops from the planning job). The top bar reads PLANNING and the feed "between sessions" (spec §7.0 rule 2).
