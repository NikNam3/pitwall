## What is drawn

- **plan-board:** a slot column (`A●` the active one, white with the bar), STOPS, E[POS] with the MC SE (`7.94±.06`), ΔPOS and ΔTIME against the active plan (positive = good; amber below −1 place), the places strip (as approved in 4.3b, `kit/dist`), an action cell. An infeasible slot is faint, its reason on hover. Below `─ DP ─` the DP's best plan per stop count (no stop, 1, 2, 3). Editing: fields for laps and compounds, `save to B` / `save as C` / `cancel`; a rejected value amber.
- **sc-card:** per block a header row naming the condition, rows stay and box S…W; E[POS]±, ΔPOS against stay, P(GAIN) against stay, REJ (from `rejoin`), the places strip; the block's best row white (its strip white).
- **strategy-overview:** one cell a lap, the white line at now; compound letters in the blocks; `²` owes its second dry compound; NEXT the likeliest next compound and its chance (odds and window on hover).
- **circle-of-doom:** our car at the top; clockwise = behind us, one lap one turn; car dots grey, LIN a white ring; ghost diamonds G/V/S, the current one white with its rejoin place and ↑ time to the car ahead, ↓ to the car behind.
