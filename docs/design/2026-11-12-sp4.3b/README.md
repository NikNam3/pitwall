## What is drawn

- **The distribution strip** (a proposed kit piece, `kit/dist`, one place in `gen.mjs`: `DIST`). One cell per bin, a bar per bin from a common baseline, heights against the panel's largest bin so rows compare. Places form (P1 … P20, header labels P1, P10, P20 above their cells): bars in the text role, white on the committed or lit row; our place now underlined white. Gain form (signed bins, seconds or places): a white zero line, bars below zero faint; in the undercut card a dotted line at the rival's gap (bars right of it end ahead).
- **strategy-table:** a bracket in the first cell joins the tied rows (shared rank, ordered by E[points]); amber with the footer's N when unresolved at the cap. SRC words `dp`, `nbr`, `rule`, `plan` (hover: the rule's name). TRG is a count, the triggers and thresholds on hover. ΔTIME (mean race time to the leader) becomes ΔPLAN with a commit (to the committed row). `commit` / `clear` are quiet buttons. Footer: `N` scenarios and `CAND` candidates.
- **undercut-card:** our own line (white bold) between the rivals ahead and behind; rival, place and gap on its N = 1 row; the gain strip per N.
- **pit-sweep-table:** the first header cell is the compound swept (hover: auto or set). LIFE an amber square. The window rows bold.
- **sc-card:** the 4.2 card's GAIN in seconds becomes ΔPOS (places, positive = good for us), P(GAIN) and the places-gained strip (−4 … +4); LOSS keeps its badge; RANGE the DP's trigger range for the next stop under that condition.
- **drivers-panel:** the two persons side by side, then LOBBY and CONFIG side by side. `config.json` values carry `ʲ` and the text role, defaults dim. No banding (two tables share each line).
- **calibration-panel:** ★ on the preferred set (text role, a click clears it), ☆ a quiet button on any other (`calibration.prefer`).

## Questions

### Q1. With a commit, what does the difference column show?

U3: the alternatives stay ranked, each with its difference to the committed row.

- **A (drawn):** ΔPLAN, mean race time against the committed row; E[POS] beside it already shows the place difference.
- **B:** ΔPLAN in places (the paired E[pos] difference), ΔTIME kept against the leader; one more column (about 95 wide).
- **C:** both differences against the committed row, the leader's ΔTIME dropped.

Recommendation: A. The ranking is in places and E[POS] shows it; time is the one difference the table does not already show.

**Answer:** A, user 2026-11-12.

### Q2. How are the distribution strips scaled?

- **A (drawn):** against the panel's largest bin: the rows compare (a flatter strip is a less certain plan).
- **B:** each row against its own largest bin: every shape is full height, heights no longer compare.

Recommendation: A. Comparing rows is the point of a table.

**Answer:** A, user 2026-11-12.

### Q3. Does the places strip mark our place now?

- **A (drawn):** a white underline under the cell of our place now (P8): bars left of it gain.
- **B:** no mark; P(GAIN) says it.

Recommendation: A. It is what the eye looks for first, and it costs no cell.

**Answer:** A, user 2026-11-12.

### Q4. How does the pit sweep show "life exceeded"?

- **A (drawn):** an amber square in LIFE when the stint passes its tyre life (the flag `pit-sweep` delivers).
- **B:** a small bar per row, the tyres' age at the stop against their life, the part past it amber (the pit-window strip's look); `pitSweep.car` then carries age and life.

Recommendation: A. The table already shows the stop lap; the bar would repeat it.

**Answer:** A, user 2026-11-12.

### Q5. Under an SC, what does the VSC row show (and the SC row under a VSC)?

Spec §9.3 computes the real condition with its remaining duration; `race-hazard` starts no other neutralisation while one is out.

- **A (round 1):** the other row keeps its "if one came now" values.
- **B (drawn, round 2):** the other row shows `–` (notApplicable, reason on hover) while one is out.

Recommendation: B. A VSC cannot start under an SC in the model, so its numbers would answer a question that cannot arise.

**Answer:** B, user 2026-11-12.

### Q6. The top-bar seat count: which seats, and how?

- **A (drawn):** our feeds' human seats without a person, as `unlinked 1` (grey label, the count in the text role), no segment at 0; a click opens the drivers panel.
- **B:** the same, the count in amber.
- **C:** every human seat of the lobby without a person (online most of them, since only our drivers have profiles, U8).

Recommendation: A. Only our seats can have profiles, so only they can be missing one; it is a setup note, not an alarm.

**Answer:** A, user 2026-11-12.

### Q7. Does the compact strategy table keep the commit buttons?

- **A (drawn):** no buttons in compact; `plan.commit` and `plan.clear` stay in the palette and the full size.
- **B:** a one-cell button per row (a glyph, hover names it).

Recommendation: A. The compact size is for watching; committing a plan wants the full row in view.

**Answer:** A, user 2026-11-12.

### Q8. How is the preferred calibration set marked?

- **A (drawn):** ★ after the picker on the preferred set, ☆ (a quiet button) on any other; ★ in the open picker.
- **B:** a `preferred` word or toggle after the picker.

Recommendation: A. One cell, and the shape says it.

**Answer:** A, user 2026-11-12.

### Q9. Strategy table: variant A or B? (round 2)

- **A (`strategy-table.html`):** one row per policy, each with its own strip; every number in view at once.
- **B (`strategy-table-b.html`):** one chart for every policy, a list beside it (rank, SRC, stops, E[POS]); a click on a line or row highlights it (white, thick, its points, a tick at its E[pos]; the others faint, the rest of its tie group grey) and shows its info rows below (TRG, E[PTS], ΔTIME or ΔPLAN, commit or clear, then its triggers and thresholds).
- **C:** B's chart above A's table: the table row click highlights its line; about 88×20.

Recommendation: A for the race, or C if the shape comparison matters. With close policies (the sample's three ties) B's lines lie on top of each other, so the chart tells them apart only once one is highlighted, and commit needs two clicks. B shows the shapes against each other better than any strip.

**Answer:** B, user 2026-11-12, after trying both; `strategy-table.html` (A) stays in the folder as the rejected variant. The workbook uses B.

### Q10. In B, how is the committed policy's line marked when nothing is highlighted?

- **A (drawn):** dashed (the theme's chart dash), grey like the others; its list row carries the bar.
- **B:** the committed line always in the highlight role (white), the clicked one then white and thick.

Recommendation: B. In the drawn form the dash is hard to find among close lines; the committed plan is the one line worth seeing without a click.

**Answer:** B, user 2026-11-12: the committed line always white at the normal width; a highlighted line white, thick and with its points.
