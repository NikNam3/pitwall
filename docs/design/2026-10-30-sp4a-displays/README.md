## Decisions

- **SP3 carries over:** `L23` is a lap number, counts say `laps`; fixed column grids with right-aligned numbers and fixed decimals; gap sign ahead negative; white bold = relevant now; amber = advisory; red = critical; compound as a grey letter; our cars white (teammate dashed in charts); numbers only, no verdicts.
- **Ghost (kit piece), B1 (Q1):** one style in the crossing order, the gap chart and the track map; always quieter than the real cars, our code plain, no glyph.
  - Crossing order: the ghost row (`12 KUH +0.612`) in `textFaint`, in the real rows' columns, between two solid 1 px `textFaint` hairlines (above and below); the condition is named by its column title.
  - Gap chart: faint (`textFaint`) 1 px lines, G dotted `[2, 3]`, VSC dashed `[7, 3]`, SC dash-dot `[7, 3, 2, 3]`, the letter (G, VSC, SC) at the line end, over the last 6 laps (Q6 A); the header names the losses (`pit G 20.3 VSC 13.1 SC 9.8`).
  - Track map: a faint hollow ring with its letter (letters dropped in compact).
- **Undercut highlight, colour (Q9 cyan):** a positive margin on an AHEAD row (our undercut possible) bold in the new role `opportunity` (`#3fd8e8`); on a BEHIND row (their undercut on us) bold `warning` amber. MARGIN = gain minus the absolute gap; on a BEHIND row the gain is what the rival gains if it stops now and we stop n laps later. STOP shows `due`.
- **Track map:** rivals as dots in their team colour (Q7 B), our cars white and larger; turn labels only from the turn file, outside each bend; car codes inside the circuit; labels never overlap each other, the dots or the line, and stay inside the panel; in-pit cars hollow; incident markers amber triangles at the topic's point, labelled with the incident kind (Q8 B).
- **Flags on the map (Q16):** a yellow marshal zone redraws its stretch of the outline in `flagYellow` (the game has no double yellow); a car under blue gets a short `flagBlue` bar (3 px × 12 px) beside its dot, on the first side (right, left, above, below) that touches no neighbouring dot, label or the line; our car under blue the same.
- **Source badge (kit piece, Q2 A):** one cell after a prior-based value: none = measured this session, `ˢ` = stored, `ᵈ` = default; every such value carries the hover (`measured · 6 stops`, `default · <note>`). Tables with room show the source in words (pit-loss table, stint compounds), the default row muted.
- **Compounds as letters (Q3 A)** from the new `visual` fields, never C-numbers.
- **Crossing order:** P = the order item's `position`, INT = `intervalMs` (Q4 A); the car directly ahead of and behind the ghost bold; compact shows the green condition, or the one that is out.
- **SC card:** one line in green running (`if SC P9 gain +10.5ᵈ · VSC P10 gain +7.2ˢ`), the sheet under SC/VSC with the active row white bold; the losses carry their source (Q5 A).
- **Pit sweep:** FINISH is the delta to the fastest row (`+0.9`, Q10 A); the `NEXT H M` selector lists the next compounds of `pitWindow.car` (Q11 A); LIFE `over` in amber.
- **Stint scatter:** used laps as dots, excluded laps as faint rings (off-scale ones pinned at the edge), down-weighted laps as dotted rings, the fit line and band from the analysis (Q12 A).
- **Raising average and lap overview** answer every car (Q13 A); the chart shows our cars white and chosen rivals in team colour.
- **Crossover:** the 110 % line from the topic (Q14 A); tyre types told apart by colour (slick text grey, inter `series3`, wet `series1`).
- **Lap overview:** heat = background brightness within the stint (`bgBand` to `textFaint`), laps outside the pace set dim, `i`/`o` marks, purple session best, green personal best, a yellow VSC/SC strip.
- **Unknown values:** empty cells with a dotted underline and the reason on hover; `notApplicable` cells blank with the hover, no underline.
- **Realism:** no field of the 4a displays is hidden for our own cars; positions stay on the map.
- **Radio window dropped** (user, round 2): the track map does the job. No `radio-clock` display.
- **Workbooks (Q15 A, a starting point):** `strategy` main = tower, crossing order, map, gap chart, undercut card, SC card, pit sweep; side = forecast strip plus the `race` workbook's car-care set (tyre life, corners, fuel, ERS, alarms, damage). `longrun` main = stint scatter, raising average, lap overview, timing tower; side = pit-loss table, crossover chart.
