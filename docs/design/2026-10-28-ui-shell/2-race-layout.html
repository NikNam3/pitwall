Race workbook: what goes where on the 24" and the 14"?

Wireframes, so look at placement, not style. The dashed top bar is fixed shell, not dockable: session, lap, flag, feed health and the newest tier-1 alert are always visible. Everything else is a dockable panel; this is only the default layout. Amber shows the escalation idea: the strategy panel lights up when the pit window opens. Click an option to select it.

ACar centre, race picture on the side

The 24" is about your driver: car state on the left two thirds, strategy decisions directly below, the cars around on the right. The 14" holds the whole field.
