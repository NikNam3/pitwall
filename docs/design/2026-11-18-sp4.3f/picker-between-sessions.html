SP4.3f · the picker between sessions

The SP4.3e picker (basis.pick) when no session runs (spec §4.4): it reads plans.sessions (every stored practice and qualifying, one entry per team). Track, then that track's qualifying per team (the team comes with it, never picked on its own; its cars shown), then practice: only sessions of the qualifying's team, several or none. No plan part (a round plans; a race chooses a plan). Preselected: the newest track, its newest qualifying, that team's practice of the same weekend. A stale session is marked; picking it rebuilds it first (the top bar shows the progress). Confirm opens the round; laps, sets and conditions are set on the plan board. In a session the picker stays SP4.3e's.
