SP4.3f · plan board: the race conditions

The planning round's first line gains the race's conditions (spec §4.3–4.4, C62d). RAIN 12% ▸ is the highest rain chance of the forecast rows; a click opens the rows in place of the plan rows (window-local, C73), one line per forecast moment the game sent for the race (minutes after the race start, its weather and rain chance). Changing a field commits one plans.race.set with the whole list; the round replans. The label after RAIN says where the rows come from: imported · Q 17 Nov while none is changed, edited after. Colour only to highlight (memory): imported rows dim, a changed row bright with its changed field outlined. Only the full size edits; compact shows the line's rain only.
