## Q1. The forecast check (U9) missed. How do we go on?

After every repair (F1–F8, the Task 11 causes, the refits; `sp4.3b/integ` 74f994c4), `pitwall score` against the F1-only base (`docs/research/17`), AI cars:

| Texas | 1.78 / 1.53 ✗ | 2.46 / 2.05 ✗ | 1,863 ms ✗ (bias +548) |
| China | 1.51 / 1.31 ✗ | 2.12 / 1.94 ✗ | 1,891 ms ✗ (bias −620) |
| Zandvoort | 0.69 / 0.59 ✗ | 0.88 / 0.94 ✓ | 1,608 ms ✗ (bias −1,210) |

The repairs fixed the decision cases (the stop-lap pace error fell from ~1 s to < 0.1 s once the double line crossing was fixed), but the plain forecast's h1 lap time is still ~1.6–2.1 s off on green laps, with a race-specific bias of ±0.5–1.2 s, so positions at h3/h5 stay worse than "gaps stay". A read-only diagnosis (2026-11-12, scratch in the session's `u9diag/`) found the lap model itself within target: on green laps from lap 4 with no stop lap the h1 error is Spa 659 ms, Texas 710, China 813, Zandvoort 464 (biases −100 to +182). Three things outside it carry the miss:

1. **Phantom rival stops** (`rival-stops`, Spa: 48 cases at −10.9 s each): it reads compound-rule `required` as "owes a stop", but `required` holds all race (it means the rule applies, not that it is unmet: C43), and a window past the peers' median age collapses onto the current lap, so cars are boxed again every lap. Fix in two phases: owes a stop = `required` and fewer than two dry compounds used; take the overdue window once the median age has passed. (The spec's §6 reads `required` the same way: a text fix.)
2. **Real stop laps scored as green laps** (`forecast-score`, Texas +804 ms of its +548 bias): a rival's actual in-lap and out-lap are not in its predicted policy, so they count as h1 lap-time misses of 7–19 s. Stop timing is already scored by the experiments.
3. **Race laps 2–3 predicted from the standing-start lap** (`forecast-field`, all of China's and Zandvoort's bias): before two pace laps the fallback pace is the lap-1 record.

- **A.** Fix 1 in two phases (a model bug, it also misplaces rivals in every Monte Carlo); change the scoreboard so a case whose horizon holds the car's own in-lap or out-lap is left out, like an SC lap (F1-like; a spec §4.2/§4.3 change), and so cases start at lap 3 (no fitted pace before); then run the check again.
- **B.** As A, but instead of leaving out laps 2–3, fix the lap-2/3 pace in `forecast-field` (never a lap-1 record; the leader's lap 2 plus a start-loss value from a spike) — more work, thin data.
- **C.** Fix only 1 and run the check again with the scoreboard as it is (stop laps and laps 2–3 still count).

Recommendation: A — 1 is a real bug; 2 and 3 measure things the forecast is not meant to predict at h1 (stop timing is scored by the experiments; a standing start is not a racing lap).

**Answer:** A (user, 2026-11-12). Fix 1 in two phases; the scoreboard leaves out cases whose horizon holds the car's own in-lap or out-lap, and cases before leader lap 3; then the check runs again. Spec §4.2 F9, F10, §4.3, §6, §14 U13.

## Q8. The rain trigger fires 9–15 % on dry Spa and Texas. Keep the chance model as it is?

Diagnosis 2026-11-13 (../pitwall-wt/.tmp/raindiag/): no defect. The forecasts carry a constant rain % all race (Spa 4 % clear, Texas 12 % light cloud, accuracy perfect). Per scenario one draw decides rain for the rest of the race (R49), so 4 % / 12 % of scenarios play a wet race, and the trigger fires in exactly those. Texas's 10–15 % is its 12 %; Spa's 9.5 % (21 of 222) and 17.5 % (7 of 40) are tail draws of a 4 % chance (the hash is unbiased over 5,000 bases). The one inconsistency: the candidate step offers inter/wet plans only from `rainCandidatePct` 25 (research 19: light rain shows from ~25 %), while the simulation reads 4 % as a literal 4 % chance (Q6 A).

- **A.** Keep it (Q6 A as decided): a small rain chance stays a small share of wet scenarios; the DP's rain trigger in the policies handles them; the fired share shown is honest about the forecast.
- **B.** Below `rainCandidatePct` a lap's chance counts as 0 in the simulation (the game's background rain % on a clear day means no rain), so both races draw no rain.
- **C.** Keep the chance but draw rain per lap block (e.g. per 5 laps) instead of once for the rest of the race, so a 4 % chance gives short showers rather than whole wet races.

Recommendation: A for now (it is what Q6 A decided and both are model choices without evidence yet); revisit with a wet recording.

**Answer:** A (user, 2026-11-13): keep the chance model; revisit with a wet recording.
