## 1. Goal

The estimation and the forecast the 4.3b decisions will run on, with no display (S9):

- driver profiles per person (C62c), linked and curated by CLI;
- four estimation calculators: `track-wetness`, `driver-noise`, `rival-stops`, `race-interaction`;

## 4. Driver profiles (bridge, C62c; T2)

**Storage.** One file per person in `PITWALL_DATA_DIR/drivers/<person>.json`: display name, confirmed links (a name and race number, or one session slot where the slot has no name), excluded sessions, overrides per entry key.

**Entries** are declared by units with a key and pure functions over the person's linked sessions (their stored projections), exclusions, overrides and the unit's config (C62c). `driver-noise` declares (§5.2): core lap scatter, lap-to-lap correlation, mistake rate, mistake size, tyre wear rate per compound (the person's own rate, never a factor against the AI, so it does not shift with the AI difficulty). Each entry is an aggregate weighted by laps and carries its number of laps and sessions and its age.

**Profile store.** A SQLite cache beside the priors store: each entry records its aggregate's version and its inputs' fingerprint; a mismatch recomputes it; deleting the store loses nothing. Only the bridge writes it, between runs, on a profile edit and after a rebuild (C61).

**Linking.** At session start the bridge applies the confirmed links to the session's slots; every other human slot gets `unknown('notApplicable')` in `view.drivers` (and with it `driver-noise`'s human default). The snapshot of the linked profiles goes to the profiles sidecar and is fixed for the run; a replay reads the sidecar; a recording without one gives `unknown('notApplicable')` for every slot. A link or an edit during a session takes effect at the next session start (C62c, C75).

**CLI `pitwall drivers`:**
- `pitwall drivers`: the persons with their entries; then every unlinked human slot of the stored sessions (date, track, team, race number, name if any) with a suggested person: from a confirmed link with the same race number, else from the team (the user: Mercedes mainly Asymetrisch, Ferrari mainly DR). A suggestion is a hint; every link is the user's (C62c).
- `pitwall drivers add <person> --name "<display name>"`;
- `pitwall drivers link <session> <slot> <person>`, or `--accept` for every shown suggestion; a link by name and race number applies to every session with that pair;
- `pitwall drivers exclude <person> <session>`, `pitwall drivers override <person> <key> <value>` (validated against the entry's declaration, rejected otherwise, never stored);
- `pitwall drivers show <person>`: each entry with its data counts, its age and each linked session's own value against the profile's.

**Topics.** `drivers.profiles` (persons, entries, counts, age) and `drivers.links` (links, unlinked slots, suggestions): core topics published by the profile module (C54, C55). The 4.3b driver-profile panel reads them.

## 5. Estimation calculators (4.3a1)

All four are quick (C31) and recompute at line crossings (a car's own for per-car outputs, the leader's for session outputs): their inputs change only there (the memory rule "faster updates if correct": a mini-sector update would repeat the same value). Each holds a behaviour table over the sub-machines it reads (C44: car activity (race), session phase (race), car status, lap validity) and reports `notApplicable` outside the race phase. Every value is a `Known` with its source (session, calibration, profile, default). Each gets a pure form "as of frame N" in its folder, which its scheduled run calls with the current frame (§6.1).

### 5.1 `track-wetness` (session)

- **Output:** per lap and tyre type (slick, inter, wet) the field's common mode (the median residual of the cars in free air on that type against their dry reference), and a filtered wetness state with its standard error.
- **Method:** a Kalman filter over laps on the gap between slick and inter times where both run, and on the common mode where one type runs; weather type and rain % enter only its forecast step, never the measurement (research 12 §3).
- It owns the common mode (C43): `driver-noise` reads it.

### 5.2 `driver-noise` (per car)

- **Output:** core scatter σ, lap-to-lap correlation ρ, mistake rate per lap, mistake size (log-normal: median, σ), big-event rate (more than 5 s or a retirement), tyre wear rate per compound.
- **Method:** `degradation`'s residuals on free-air laps (no car within the config's free-air gap ahead), minus `track-wetness`'s common mode; σ robust (median absolute deviation × 1.4826); ρ from consecutive residuals. AI mistakes: free-air laps beyond 3σ on the slow side. Human mistakes: contact, damage jumps and time-loss incidents, through a shared pure function from the incidents logic in `analysis/src/shared/` (C45), not from the lap-time tail (research 12 §2).
- **Shrinkage:** a human starts from its profile (`view.drivers`), else the human default; an AI car from the field default, adjusted to this session by the AI field's own spread; each moves towards its session values as laps come in (shrinkage as `pit-loss`, strength in config). Mistake laps are left out of the σ.
- **Profile entries:** §4. The AI rate is per field (S7).

### 5.3 `rival-stops` (per rival)

- **Output:** the next stop's window (low, middle, high lap), the odds of each next compound, whether the car still owes a stop (the compound rule's `required` span).
- **Method:** the tyre age at which this race's peers stopped, per compound (median and spread); before any peer has stopped, `tyre-life`'s predicted life; both capped by the laps left. A car that has used its sets or owes no stop gets an open window to the flag.

### 5.4 `race-interaction` (track and session)

- **Output:** the pass curve per difficulty band (a logistic per braking zone in pace advantage with its cap, a wet factor), the DRS pass factor and the DRS straight gain (relative: against the car directly ahead, so gains cancel inside a train), the minimum following gap, the time the car ahead loses while a faster car is within it, the rejoin push-back, dirty air (0 by default).
- **Sources:** the pooled default from the fit (§5.5) in `config.ts` with its source; refined by the track's calibration set through a priors entry over its stints' following episodes (C62a); refined within the race by this race's episodes with shrinkage.
- **Priors entries** (C62a): the pass curve's level and slope, with `aggregate`, `residual`, `candidate` as `pit-loss` declares its entries.

### 5.5 The pooled pass-curve fit (T3, T4)

A throwaway script on a branch that is never merged (as the spikes), driving the engine as `scan-situations` does, with its filter as tested code in the script:

- **Sessions:** race sessions with at least 10 completed laps and a classification. Out: menu and lobby recordings, the time trial, qualifying, practice, the abandoned and 0-lap races.
- **Laps:** out are lap 1; SC, VSC and formation laps; in- and out-laps and passes caused by a stop; retired cars; cars with aero damage of 10 % or more; the simulated end after our retirement. Wet and dry laps apart (a wet term).
- **AI difficulty** (`aiDifficulty`, in the session's live state): a term in the fit; a curve per difficulty band if the data separates the bands (today 90: Singapore, China; 62–70: Mexico, Miami, Texas, Zandvoort), else one curve with the range in its source (T4).
- **Result:** the curve's parameters with their data counts (races, episodes, passes) and the races and laps used, written to `race-interaction/config.ts` as the default with its source; a calibration set shows each source race's difficulty.
- **O14 checks:** whether the car ahead loses time when a faster car is within the minimum gap (TUM charges both 0.3 s per lap), and the rejoin push-back (TUM 0.5 s, Aguad 0.4 s); the results set the two config defaults.

## 6. The forecast (4.3a2)

### 6.1 `forecast-field` (`analysis/src/shared/forecast-field/`)

The only code that turns the view into a starting point (C50, T6).

- **Input:** the projection cut at frame N (the facts describing frames up to N, through the projection module's membership, C26, C28) and, on the live path, the current frame's lap progress. The scoreboard passes a past crossing frame; the live path the current frame.
- **Output (plain typed arrays, one entry per car):** order, gap, lap fraction; compound, tyre age, wear %, sets left (our cars), fuel; pace level and degradation rate, each with its σ (from `degradation`'s posterior covariance of `(α, ν)`: the pace uncertainty, S25); the noise parameters (`driver-noise`); predicted stops (`rival-stops`); wetness (`track-wetness`); retired. The track: braking zones and DRS zones (track file), pit loss per condition (`pit-loss`), the interaction parameters (`race-interaction`).
- **Realism:** where a rival's wear is `unknown('realism')`, tyre age × that car's wear rate (C8).
- **Property:** at every crossing frame the "as of" build equals the live build.

### 6.2 `lap-model` (simulator)

One car, one lap, additive (high-level §3.2): base pace; the fresh-set compound offset (fitted from laps, the track file a weak prior); the tyre term per % wear, linear, the convex shape a flagged prior beyond about 40 %; fuel 0.03 s/kg (fixed prior); the cold-tyre terms for the first two laps on a set (larger in the wet); the neutralised terms (SC, VSC: slower, less wear and fuel); the wetness term per tyre type; the scenario's scatter and mistake draws.

### 6.3 `race-forecast` (simulator)

The field to the flag under one scenario and one policy per car; positions, gaps, race times and an optional per-lap trace out.

- **Steps:** every car from braking zone to braking zone (S25); a follower held at the minimum gap (blocking); at each braking zone a pass chance from `race-interaction`'s curve; DRS relative, its pass factor only where the car ahead is not itself in DRS range, its straight gain for every car in range.
- **Events:** stops with the condition's pit loss, the new set, cold tyres, a double-stack wait (`max(0, 6.5 s − entry gap)`, research 12 §5) and the stationary time per driver type; retirements; SC and VSC as scenario inputs (the field queues; nobody passes under VSC); their draws come in 4.3b.
- **Draws:** streams keyed by (scenario, car, lap, purpose), hashed from the injected generator (C2); pace level and degradation rate drawn per scenario around the builder's σ.
- **Plain mode** (no draws): a held car passes at the median pass time (research 13 §10).

### 6.4 The test-race generator (`test-support/race-generator/`)

Written independently of the simulators (not circular): races of 20 cars from known parameters (degradation per compound, pit loss, pace levels, each driver's scatter, correlation, mistakes and wear rate, the pass curve, DRS, SC timing, rain), stepped in continuous time with a pass hazard over time rather than per braking zone. Its output is facts and live states that the existing builders (`test-support/builders`) turn into a view, plus the true parameters for the recovery tests. Seeded; no clock.

## 7. The scoreboard (T5, T6, T7)

### 7.1 `forecast-score` (analysis with a job)

On demand (C49): it runs only while `forecastScore.report` is subscribed. A job (C51, T7) triggered at the leader's crossing: it rebuilds the starting points of the earlier laps whose horizons matured (about 4 horizons × the laps so far: about 280 plain forecasts at lap 70) and scores them; no state between runs (C31). The plain forecast is deterministic, so the job's exclusion from the determinism hash loses nothing.

**Natural experiments** (O12, tested code; dry laps only, weather crossovers excluded, research 12 §8):
- **Rejoin:** every green stop: the forecast at the in-lap crossing with "box this lap" against the position and gap at the first crossing after the out-lap. Out: repairs (more than 6 s stationary), penalty stops.
- **Undercut and overcut:** two cars within 3 s at the first stopper's in-lap crossing, stopping 1 to 5 laps apart, no SC or VSC between, not a team double stack: the predicted change of their gap (both actual stop laps) against the change measured after the second car's out-lap.
- **SC stop:** every stop under SC or VSC against the cars within ±3 places that stayed out: the predicted gain against the actual change two laps after the neutralisation ends.

**Forecasts:** per lap L and horizon h ∈ {1, 3, 5, 10} laps: the plain forecast's lap-time and position error, beside the "gaps stay as they are" baseline, split by basis (wear, age).

Every table split AI and human, with its number of cases. No targets (S2).

### 7.2 `pitwall score <recording>` (bridge CLI)

Reprocesses the recording, subscribes to `forecastScore.report`, waits for the final job result and prints the tables; `--json <file>` writes them; `--compare <old.json>` prints each number beside the old one with its change (T5). Nothing is stored by pitwall (C32).

## 9. Testing (`docs/testing.md`)

- **Calculators:** recovery of the generator's parameters within its noise; behaviour-table completeness (C44); guarded views (C41); determinism, rewind exactness, shuffle within a step (C86); "as of frame N" equals the scheduled run at every crossing frame; `notApplicable` outside the race phase.
- **Simulators** (high-level §7.1): no stops and equal pace keep gaps; a pass chance of 0 means no pass, and the chance never falls as the pace advantage grows; a stop never gains a position at the rejoin; an extra stop never shortens the plain race time; one more stop of one car leaves every other draw unchanged; the plain forecast reproduces a generated race within its noise; a seeded run is bit-identical.
- **Natural experiments:** each definition on builder cases; segment tests on real data for one behaviour each (a pass at a braking zone, a rejoin), cut with `pwrec-cut`, at most 1 MB (C84).
- **Driver profiles:** store, fingerprint and rebuild (as the priors store's tests), linking and suggestions, the CLI, against a temporary data dir; profiles passed explicitly (C84).
- **`pitwall score`:** on a committed segment, the CLI's output shape, `--json` and `--compare`.
- **Reviews:** after every task the one question (C89).
