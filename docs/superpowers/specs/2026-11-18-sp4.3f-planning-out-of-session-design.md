## 1. Intent

The user, 2026-11-18 (restating what they told the SP4.3e spec writer):

> There are two cases:
> **A, the easy case.** One session: practice, quali, race / sprint, one evening, about 4 hours; planning happens between quali and the race with the session active.
> **B, the new case.** Up to 3 sessions: practice; quali; race / sprint. Planning happens anywhere between quali and race / sprint **out of session**.
>
> Quali needs to know a corresponding practice, and the links between which in-game car belongs to which driver profile.
> Planning needs to know a corresponding practice, which delivers the weekend per driver and tyre data, and a corresponding quali, which delivers the grid order, and the driver profiles linked to the in-game cars. That's it. The user then selects the practice, the quali, the weather forecast / track conditions, the cars to plan for, and gets the UI they plan with. In session (case A) all those picks are preselected.
> Race / sprint need to know a corresponding quali (the grid order), a corresponding practice (the weekend per driver and tyre data), the links between in-game cars and driver profiles, and a plan file.

So, per kind of work:

| Work | Needs | The user picks |
|---|---|---|
| Qualifying | a practice; the car → driver-profile links | (preselected) |
| Planning | a practice (per-driver weekend and tyre data); a qualifying (grid order); the car → driver-profile links | practice, qualifying, weather forecast / track conditions, the cars to plan for; then plans with the board. Case A: all preselected. |
| Race / sprint | a qualifying (grid); a practice (per-driver and tyre data); the car → profile links; a saved plan | (preselected, changeable: SP4.3e's picker) |

**The team is handed forward** (user, 2026-11-18): every stored session already records our team (the feed's player car and teammate, with team and race number per car slot, C62). The practice and the qualifying of a round must be **of the same team**, and a round plans **only for that team's cars**. The team comes from the picked sessions, never from a feed or a remembered seat.

## 3. Goals, non-goals, anti-goals

**Goals**
- G1. **Case B in the panel.** On a bridge that has seen no session (fresh start, no `feeds/` files), the user opens the picker from the top bar. They pick a track's qualifying, its practice(s), the cars to plan for, the conditions, the race laps and the sets. Then they plan on the plan board (slots, active slot) and save the plan. Nothing reads a remembered seat.
- G2. **Case A unchanged in effect.** At a qualifying's live end the round opens with every pick preselected: that qualifying, its basis's practice, the feed's player car and teammate, the qualifying's forecast for the race, the suggested laps and `carried`. The same picker changes any pick.
- G3. **The picked sessions define the team.** The round's team is the picked qualifying's stored team (its player car's). Every picked practice must be stored for the same team; otherwise the bridge rejects the pick. The round plans only that team's cars. The saved plans' scope and the last-choice record follow from that team and the qualifying's track, not from a feed.
- G4. **The conditions are a race assumption.** The user's weather forecast / track conditions replace the qualifying's forecast in the round's view, as the laps and sets already do (C62d). They are preselected from the qualifying's forecast for the race.
- G5. **The round uses the current car → profile links** for the picked cars.
- G6. **The race needs nothing new.** It keeps SP4.3e's picker (basis and saved plan), preselection and plan decisions.
- G7. **An end-to-end test of case B in the panel:** a bridge with no session and a temporary data dir with an imported practice and qualifying. The test opens the picker, picks, sees the board ranked for the picked cars, saves, restarts and sees the saved plan.

**Non-goals**
- Planning across tracks; mixing a practice of one track with a qualifying of another.
- Planning on a race (a basis of a race session).
- New weather modelling: the conditions feed the existing `weather-paths` and `rain-outlook` as the qualifying's forecast does today.
- Track temperature as a planning input. Today no planner reads it; if the user wants it (Q2), it is recorded for later.
- The SP4.3e open questions Q1, Q3–Q5 (run log): they stay as they are.

**Anti-goals** (a failure even if it works)
- Anything between sessions that depends on which feed last saw which car (a remembered seat), or on `session.info`.
- Two pickers: the between-sessions picker is SP4.3e's picker with more parts, not a second one.
- A planning input the user cannot see in the top bar or on the board (C38: every assumption is shown and stored with the saved plan).
- A conditions value of 0 % for "not entered" (C3): unknown stays unknown and is shown as missing.

## 4. Design

### 4.0 The concepts

1. **A planning round is a function of the picks** (C62d, widened): a qualifying stored for a team, a practice basis stored for the same team, the cars of that team to plan for and the race assumptions `{ laps, sets, conditions }`. The team is handed forward from the stored sessions; it is never chosen separately or read from a feed.
2. **The stored sessions are bridge state, not a feed's.** A new core topic lists every stored practice and qualifying by track: date, weekend, its cars with team and race number, stale or current. The between-sessions picker reads it. No seat is needed to list it.
3. **The round's cars give the panels their link** between sessions: the shell resolves a link group's `player` and `teammate` to the open round's first and second picked car on the round's feed. Each link is the car's slot id in the qualifying, the key `plans.preview` and `plans.prepared` already use.
4. **Case A is case B with preselected picks.**

### 4.1 The contract change (C0: first, with the ADR line, before any code)

To be worded on approval, in the contract's style. The intent:
- **Glossary:**
  - "race assumptions": the race laps, whether the race's tyre sets carry over from the qualifying or are a fresh allocation, and the race's weather forecast (the conditions), entered for a planning round (C62d).
  - "planning round": the cars it plans for are part of its definition.
- **C62d:**
  - The round is a function of a qualifying recording, a practice basis **of the same team** (the team of each stored session's player car), **the cars of that team to plan for** (by race number, C1a) and the race assumptions. A practice of another team is rejected.
  - Its as-of view takes the race assumptions' **forecast in place of the qualifying's** (as it takes the laps, and for fresh sets the sets).
  - Its profile snapshot is **the current snapshot of the picked cars' linked drivers**, not the recording's profiles sidecar.
  - At a qualifying's live end, the round opens with the feed's player car and teammate, and its forecast is the qualifying's forecast for the race.
  - The round's team, and so its saved plans and last choice, is the picked qualifying's team.
- **C54:** the stored sessions per track (the new topic of §4.2) are bridge user state published as a core topic.
- **C62 / R19:** the remembered feed seat is deleted. Nothing between sessions reads a feed's last seat.

### 4.2 Bridge

- **The stored-sessions topic.** A core topic, e.g. `plans.sessions` (name the builder's), published by the planning module.
  - Content: per track, every stored practice and qualifying (and sprint shootout), each listed once with its date and kind.
  - The same session can be stored once per feed (one row per player car); the topic merges them by `sessionUid/weekendId`.
  - Each session is listed **per team it is stored for** (the player car's team of each stored row), with that team's cars (race number, human or AI), its other car slots with team and race number, and stale or current. On a weekend where our drivers race for two teams, a qualifying appears once per team.
  - It is published at bridge start from storage, without rebuilding anything (C61), and again after a rebuild or a new stored session.
  - It replaces the remembered seat's role in listing candidates.
- **Opening a round.** `plans.round.open` takes `{ feedId, qualifying, practice[], cars: CarKey[], race: { laps, sets, conditions } }`.
  - Its `feedId` is only the topic address (the window's default feed). It decides nothing about the round.
  - `cars` are race numbers of the team, not free `CarKey`s.
  - The bridge checks the picks:
    - the qualifying is stored for a team (its player car's); that team is the round's;
    - every picked practice is stored for the same team, on the qualifying's track;
    - every car is one of that team's cars in the qualifying;
    - laps are within `MAX_RACE_LAPS`;
    - the conditions are valid.

    An invalid pick is rejected (C73).
- **The recording to replay.** A qualifying stored by both of the team's feeds has one recording per feed. The round replays the one whose player car is the first picked car; without one, the team's other one. Realism (C8) is then the same as in that car's race. The builder checks that a teammate's values are visible in the other's recording, and reports if not. A recording of another team is never used.
- **Team, last choice and saved plans** follow from the picked qualifying's team. `feed-seats.ts`, `seatOf`'s remembered fallback and the hand-seeded `feeds/` files are deleted. Each deletion needs a test, ported or deleted with the mechanism (C82).
- **Profiles.** The round builds the profile snapshot of the picked cars' linked drivers, as a session start does (C62c), and hands it to the replay in place of the recording's profiles sidecar.
- **Case A.** At a qualifying's live end, `sessionEnded` opens the round with all of these:
  - cars: the feed's player car and teammate;
  - conditions: the qualifying's forecast for the race;
  - laps: the suggested laps;
  - sets: `carried`.

  This is today's behaviour, with the new fields filled.

### 4.3 Analysis

- The planning input (`packages/analysis/src/shared/strategy-input/planning.ts`):
  - plans the round's picked cars, not `ourCars(playerCar, teammateCar)`;
  - takes the round's conditions as the race's forecast in place of the qualifying's `nextForecast`.

  The one builder (`buildGridStart`, C50) is what forwards them to the weather worlds (`weather-paths`) and the rain filter (`rain-outlook`). The candidates' rain filter and `rainPct` follow from the same forecast.
- The shape of the conditions follows Q2. The default, if the user agrees: the game's forecast shape, a list of `{ offsetMin | lap, rainPct }`, preselected from the qualifying's samples for the race.

### 4.4 UI

- **The picker between sessions** (`BasisPicker.svelte`; shell, supervisor-owned, C78) reads the stored-sessions topic. Its parts, each preselected:
  - **track:** the newest stored qualifying's track, or the last choice's;
  - **qualifying:** one, listed per team it is stored for (so the team is picked with it, never separately);
  - **practice:** several, or none; only sessions stored for the qualifying's team are offered;
  - **cars:** the qualifying's team's cars (its player car and teammate), both preselected; unticking one plans only the other;
  - **conditions:** the race forecast;
  - **laps** and **sets**.

  Confirm sends `plans.round.open`. In a session it stays SP4.3e's basis-and-plan picker, unchanged.
- **The top bar** shows the open round between sessions, e.g. `Planning · Practice 17 Nov · Qualifying 17 Nov · 56 laps · fresh · rain 0–10 %`. Without a round it shows a quiet `Plan…` button that runs the picker's command. The command exists with no session too (C72).
- **Link resolution** (`resolve.ts`): with no session and an open round on the default feed, `player` and `teammate` resolve to the round's first and second picked car on the round's feed. The board, the strategy table and the overview of the `plan` workbook then show the round. With a session, the session decides, as today.
- **The layout controller** (C67) gives the `plan` workbook to "between sessions with an open round", as a switch rule in UI state. It can be pinned or overridden as always.
- **The plan board** keeps its controls. Laps and sets stay editable there; the conditions are editable in the picker (or on the board, the builder's choice, one place only).
