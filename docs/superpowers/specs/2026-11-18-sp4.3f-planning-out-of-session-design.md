## 1. Intent

The user, 2026-11-18 (restating what they told the SP4.3e spec writer):

> There are two cases:
> **A, the easy case.** One session: practice, quali, race / sprint, one evening, about 4 hours; planning happens between quali and the race with the session active.
> **B, the new case.** Up to 3 sessions: practice; quali; race / sprint. Planning happens anywhere between quali and race / sprint **out of session**.
>
> Quali needs to know a corresponding practice, and the links between which in-game car belongs to which driver profile.
> Planning needs to know a corresponding practice, which delivers the weekend per driver and tyre data, and a corresponding quali, which delivers the grid order, and the driver profiles linked to the in-game cars. That's it. The user then selects the practice, the quali, the weather forecast / track conditions, the cars to plan for, and gets the UI they plan with. In session (case A) all those picks are preselected.
> Race / sprint need to know a corresponding quali (the grid order), a corresponding practice (the weekend per driver and tyre data), the links between in-game cars and driver profiles, and a plan file.

So, per kind of work:

| Work | Needs | The user picks |
|---|---|---|
| Qualifying | a practice; the car → driver-profile links | (preselected) |
| Planning | a practice (per-driver weekend and tyre data); a qualifying (grid order); the car → driver-profile links | practice, qualifying, weather forecast / track conditions, the cars to plan for; then plans with the board. Case A: all preselected. |
| Race / sprint | a qualifying (grid); a practice (per-driver and tyre data); the car → profile links; a saved plan | (preselected, changeable: SP4.3e's picker) |

**The team is handed forward** (user, 2026-11-18): every stored session already records our team (the feed's player car and teammate, with team and race number per car slot, C62). The practice and the qualifying of a round must be **of the same team**, and a round plans **only for that team's cars**. The team comes from the picked sessions, never from a feed or a remembered seat.

**Cars are identified by team and race number** (C62), never by slot. Verified on the stored Texas sessions: in the 17 Nov practice and qualifying our cars are slots 0 and 1 (Mercedes #64 and #2). In the 18 Nov race, in a new lobby, they are slots 18 and 19, still Mercedes #64 and #2. A car that changes team or race number between sessions is another car. The user accepts that planning does not carry across such a change (user, 2026-11-18: practising in the Mercedes and racing the Ferraris is not supported).

## 3. Goals, non-goals, anti-goals

**Goals**
- G1. **Case B in the panel.** On a bridge that has seen no session (fresh start, no `feeds/` files), the user opens the picker from the top bar. They pick a track's qualifying (which brings its team and so the cars) and its practice(s). On the plan board (Ctrl+O to the `plan` workbook) they set the race laps, the sets and the conditions, plan (slots, active slot) and save the plan. Nothing reads a remembered seat.
- G2. **Case A unchanged in effect.** At a qualifying's live end the round opens with every pick preselected: that qualifying, its basis's practice, its team's cars, the qualifying's forecast for the race, the suggested laps and `carried`. The same picker changes any pick.
- G3. **The picked sessions define the team.** The round's team is the picked qualifying's stored team (its player car's). Every picked practice must be stored for the same team; otherwise the bridge rejects the pick. The round plans that team's cars, by team and race number, as the qualifying stored them (its player car and teammate). The user does not pick cars. The saved plans' scope and the last-choice record follow from that team and the qualifying's track, not from a feed.
- G4. **The conditions are a race assumption.** The qualifying's forecast for the race (the game's samples for the race session, the best source there is, user 2026-11-18) is handed to the planner as the conditions' default; the user overrides it on the plan board, next to laps and sets. The conditions replace the qualifying's forecast in the round's view, as the laps and sets already do (C62d). Without a race forecast in the qualifying they are unknown and shown as missing until entered (C3).

  **What the imported forecast is worth** (checked on the recordings, 2026-11-18). The game sends the race's samples (session type 15) in every practice and qualifying:
  - **Same lobby: it is the race's forecast.** On 4 Nov the qualifying's race samples and the race's own forecast at its start were identical (light cloud, 12 % from +0 to +30 min). On 10 Nov the practice's race samples matched the race's start to within 1 %.
  - **New lobby: it is only a guess.** A new lobby rolls its own weather. The 17 Nov qualifying said 1 % for the race. The 18 Nov race, in a new lobby, had its own 3–4 %. No recording made before the lobby exists can know its weather.

  > **Note for the user, to show on the plan board and in the live checklist.** If the race runs in a new lobby and its weather was not set to match the practice and qualifying, the planner's imported forecast (from practice or qualifying) is wrong. The user planning must override the conditions on the plan board with the new lobby's weather. Once the race session runs, its own forecast reaches the race strategy at its first packets (`forecast-field/index.ts:443` reads the race's `forecast`), so the in-race strategy is correct either way. Only the plan made beforehand rests on the override.
- G5. **The round uses the current car → profile links** for the picked cars.
- G6. **The race needs nothing new.** It keeps SP4.3e's picker (basis and saved plan), preselection and plan decisions.
- G7. **An end-to-end test of case B in the panel:** a bridge with no session and a temporary data dir with an imported synthetic practice and qualifying (§4.5). The test opens the picker, picks, sees the board ranked for the team's cars, saves, restarts and sees the saved plan, all within its speed budget.

**Non-goals**
- Planning across tracks; mixing a practice of one track with a qualifying of another.
- Planning on a race (a basis of a race session).
- New weather modelling: the conditions feed the existing `weather-paths` and `rain-outlook` as the qualifying's forecast does today.
- Track temperature as a planning input. Today no planner reads it; it is recorded for later.
- Automatic workbook switching to the `plan` workbook: decided against by the user (2026-11-18). The user switches (Ctrl+O).
- The SP4.3e open questions Q1, Q3–Q5 (run log): they stay as they are.

**Anti-goals** (a failure even if it works)
- Anything between sessions that depends on which feed last saw which car (a remembered seat), or on `session.info`.
- Two pickers: the between-sessions picker is SP4.3e's picker with more parts, not a second one.
- A planning input the user cannot see in the top bar or on the board (C38: every assumption is shown and stored with the saved plan).
- A conditions value of 0 % for "not entered" (C3): unknown stays unknown and is shown as missing.

## 4. Design

### 4.0 The concepts

1. **A planning round is a function of the picks** (C62d, widened): a qualifying stored for a team, a practice basis stored for the same team and the race assumptions `{ laps, sets, conditions }`. It plans that team's cars, by team and race number. The team is handed forward from the stored sessions; it is never chosen separately or read from a feed.
2. **The stored sessions are bridge state, not a feed's.** A new core topic lists every stored practice and qualifying by track: date, weekend, its cars with team and race number, stale or current. The between-sessions picker reads it. No seat is needed to list it.
3. **The round's cars give the panels their link** between sessions: the shell resolves a link group's `player` and `teammate` to the round's player car and teammate (the qualifying's) on the round's feed. The round publishes each car with its team, race number and its slot in the replayed qualifying recording. The slot is the key `plans.preview` and `plans.prepared` already use, and only the bridge maps team and race number to it.
4. **Case A is case B with preselected picks.**

### 4.1 The contract change (C0: first, with the ADR line, before any code)

To be worded on approval, in the contract's style. The intent:
- **Glossary:**
  - "race assumptions" (changed): the race laps, whether the race's tyre sets carry over from the qualifying or are a fresh allocation, and the race's forecast (the conditions: the game's forecast samples for the race), entered for a planning round (C62d).
  - "planning round" (new entry; today C62d defines it without one): a function of a qualifying, a practice basis of the same team and the race assumptions; it plans that team's cars.
- **C62d:**
  - The round is a function of a qualifying recording, a practice basis **of the same team** (the team of each stored session's player car) and the race assumptions. It plans **that team's cars**, the qualifying's player car and teammate, by team and race number (C62, C1a). A practice of another team is rejected.
  - Its as-of view takes the race assumptions' **conditions in place of the qualifying's `nextForecast`** (as it takes the laps, and for fresh sets the sets).
  - Its profile snapshot is **the current snapshot of the team's cars' linked drivers**, not the recording's profiles sidecar.
  - At a qualifying's live end the round opens with that qualifying's team, and its conditions are the qualifying's forecast for the race.
  - The round's team, and so its saved plans and last choice, is the picked qualifying's team.
  - A saved plan also notes, for display only, the conditions it was computed on and the fingerprint of its profile snapshot.
- **C54:** the stored sessions per track (the new topic of §4.2) are bridge user state published as a core topic.

The remembered feed seat (R19, `feed-seats.ts`) appears in no clause, so deleting it is a code task (§4.2, §4.6 task 3), not part of the contract change.

### 4.2 Bridge

- **The stored-sessions topic.** A core topic, e.g. `plans.sessions` (name the builder's), published by the planning module.
  - Content: per track, every stored practice and qualifying (and sprint shootout), each listed once with its date and kind.
  - The same session can be stored once per feed (one row per player car); the topic merges them by `sessionUid/weekendId`.
  - Each session is listed **per team it is stored for** (the player car's team of each stored row), with that team's cars (race number, human or AI), its other car slots with team and race number, and stale or current. On a weekend where our drivers race for two teams, a qualifying appears once per team.
  - It is published at bridge start from storage, without rebuilding anything (C61), and again after a rebuild or a new stored session.
  - It replaces the remembered seat's role in listing candidates.
- **Opening a round.** `plans.round.open` takes `{ feedId, qualifying: { ref, team }, practice[], race: { laps, sets, conditions } }`. It has no cars: they follow from the qualifying's team.
  - Its `feedId` is only the topic address (the window's default feed). It decides nothing about the round. Between sessions a window on another of the team's feeds does not see the round. This is intended (C1a: topics are per feed): it opens its own.
  - The bridge checks the picks:
    - the qualifying is stored for the named team (its player car's); that team is the round's;
    - every picked practice is stored for the same team, on the qualifying's track;
    - laps are within `MAX_RACE_LAPS`;
    - the conditions are valid: offsets rising and within the race's samples' range, rain 0–100, a known weather.

    An invalid pick is rejected (C73).
  - **A stale pick waits.** A picked session whose rules version is stale is rebuilt first (C61, one rebuild at a time, off the thread that applies frames). The round's state is `rebuilding` until every pick is current, and the top bar shows it. A running session never waits for it.
- **The recording to replay.** A qualifying stored by both of the team's feeds has one recording per feed. The round replays the one of its player car's feed; without it, the teammate's. Realism hides only rivals (`state-update/realism.ts:61` unmasks the player and the teammate), so either recording shows both of the team's cars. A recording of another team is never used.
- **Team, last choice and saved plans** follow from the picked qualifying's team. `feed-seats.ts`, `seatOf`'s remembered fallback and the hand-seeded `feeds/` files are deleted. Each deletion needs a test, ported or deleted with the mechanism (C82).
- **Profiles.** The round builds the profile snapshot of the team's cars' linked drivers, as a session start does (C62c), and hands it to the replay in place of the recording's profiles sidecar.
- **Case A.** At a qualifying's live end, `sessionEnded` opens the round with all of these:
  - team: the qualifying's;
  - conditions: the qualifying's forecast for the race, its last samples;
  - laps: the suggested laps;
  - sets: `carried`.

  This is today's behaviour, with the new fields filled.

### 4.3 Analysis

- The planning input (`packages/analysis/src/shared/strategy-input/planning.ts`):
  - plans the round's cars (team and race number, mapped to the recording's slots by the bridge);
  - reads the race's forecast only from the as-of view's `nextForecast`, which the round has replaced with its conditions (§4.1).

  The one builder (`buildGridStart`, C50) is what forwards it to every reader of the race's forecast:
  - the weather worlds (`weather-paths`): **fix `forecast-field/index.ts:303`**, which reads the qualifying's own `forecast`, to read the race's forecast as line 443 does;
  - the rain filter (`rain-outlook`), and through it the candidates' rain filter and `rainPct`;
  - `track-wetness` (`config.ts`, `asof.ts`), which reads `nextForecast` for the race's starting wetness.
- **The conditions' shape** (Q2): the game's own forecast list, unchanged, as domain's `ForecastSample` already holds it. In plain terms, a short list of moments after the race start, each with a weather and a rain chance:

  ```
  +0 min    clear         1 %
  +5 min    clear         1 %
  +15 min   clear         1 %
  +30 min   light cloud  12 %
  +60 min   light rain   45 %
  …         (the game sends up to +120 min)
  ```

  It is preselected from the qualifying's last race samples. The user changes the weather or the rain chance of any row. The temperatures of a row stay the game's and are not edited (track temperature is no planning input, §3). `forecast-field` already maps the moments onto laps by the expected lap time. Without race samples the list is `unknown('notReceived')` until the user enters it (C3). Units: see Q5.

### 4.4 UI

- **The picker between sessions** (`BasisPicker.svelte`; shell, supervisor-owned, C78) reads the stored-sessions topic. Its parts, each preselected:
  - **track:** the newest stored qualifying's track, or the last choice's;
  - **qualifying:** one, listed per team it is stored for (so the team is picked with it, never separately);
  - **practice:** several, or none; only sessions stored for the qualifying's team are offered;
  - the team's cars are shown with the qualifying (team, race numbers, names), not picked.

  A session whose rules version is stale is marked as such; picking it is allowed and the round waits for its rebuild (§4.2). Confirm sends `plans.round.open` with the race assumptions' defaults: the suggested laps, `carried`, the qualifying's race forecast (or, reopening the same qualifying, the round's last entered values). The race assumptions are not in the picker. In a session it stays SP4.3e's basis-and-plan picker, unchanged.
- **The top bar** shows the open round between sessions, e.g. `Planning · Practice 17 Nov · Qualifying 17 Nov · 56 laps · fresh · rain 0–10 %`. Without a round it shows a quiet `Plan…` button that runs the picker's command. The command exists with no session too (C72).
- **Link resolution** (`resolve.ts`): with no session and an open round on the default feed, `player` and `teammate` resolve to the round's player car and teammate (their slots, as the round publishes them) on the round's feed. The board, the strategy table and the overview of the `plan` workbook then show the round. With a session, the session decides, as today.
- **No automatic workbook switch** (user, 2026-11-18): the user opens the `plan` workbook (Ctrl+O); no switch rule is added.
- **The plan board** edits every race assumption in one place: laps, sets and, new, the conditions. The conditions are the forecast rows of §4.3: weather and rain chance per row, each overridable, plus a reset to the imported forecast. Each edit is one `plans.race.set` uiAction, validated by the bridge (C73), and recomputes the round as laps and sets do today. The board shows:
  - which rows are overridden;
  - while no row is overridden, that the conditions are imported, and from which session and date (e.g. `imported · Qualifying 17 Nov`). Pitwall cannot know whether the race will be in a new lobby, so this label is the reminder of G4's note. The board's `text.ts` holds the wording. It is a short label, not an explaining sentence: no explaining text in displays. The full note goes in the live checklist (§4.6 task 9).

### 4.5 Tests

The round needs a replay of its qualifying to its end, and real recordings are large (the Texas practice fixture is 74 MB). Real recordings are allowed only as segments of about 8 MB at most (C84), so the tests are split by what they check, and no test replays a real session whole:

- **Logic, no replay** (unit tests on hand-built views from `test-support/builders`):
  - the round as a function of the picks and conditions;
  - the team check (another team's practice is rejected);
  - cars by team and race number across different slots;
  - conditions replacing `nextForecast`, the `forecast-field:303` fix, `track-wetness` reading the conditions;
  - profiles from the current links;
  - the remembered seat gone.
- **The bridge's round, tiny synthetic recordings.** As `apps/bridge/test/planning/planning-runner.test.ts` already does, the test encodes a few hundred packets (Session with chosen race samples, Participants, LapData, the session's end) and writes them with `PwrecWriter`. That gives a qualifying of one lap and a practice of a few laps, of team X with chosen race numbers, a second recording of them in other slots, and one of another team. They replay in milliseconds, and the test controls the data exactly: e.g. 40 % rain at +30 min in the race samples, asserted as the board's default conditions. The builders for these recordings go to `test-support/` (shared, built before the tasks that use them, C81).
- **E2E (`apps/ui/e2e/`, new spec), on the same synthetic recordings:**
  - a temporary data dir: `pitwall import` of the synthetic practice and qualifying;
  - a bridge with no UDP traffic;
  - open the panel;
  - no session, so no `session.info`;
  - the picker lists the track's sessions;
  - pick the qualifying and practice, confirm;
  - switch to the `plan` workbook;
  - the board shows the ranked plans for the team's two cars, with the qualifying's race samples as the conditions;
  - override a forecast row on the board: the plans are computed again;
  - save the plan;
  - restart the bridge: the saved plan is listed and preselected at a race start (a synthetic race start of the same team, a replay).

  The test sets few race laps (e.g. 5) so the planning job is short. It needs its own UDP port: SP4.3e's stray recording came from a test fixture sending to 20777.
  **Speed budget:** the whole spec runs in at most 60 s on the dev machine, and each wait for a round's result in at most 15 s. A miss is profiled and fixed, never met by raising a timeout.
- **Real data, one segment test (optional, not in the acceptance gate):** the 4 Nov one-shot qualifying fixture (8 MB, one lap) checks that step 1's `nextForecast` holds the race samples of a real qualifying. It asserts only that they exist and have session type 15, not their values (C84).
- **Case A regression:** at a qualifying's end in a replay, the round opens with every pick preselected, and the panel shows it.
- **Determinism** (C38): a round's result for the same picks is the same across runs (job topics excepted as today). A race replayed with a saved plan made from a round with entered conditions gives the same log.
