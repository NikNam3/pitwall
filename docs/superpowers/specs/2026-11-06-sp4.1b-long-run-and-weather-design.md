## 1. Goal

Four judgements the engineer reads in long runs and changing weather, each with its display, in the `garage` workbook:

- **raising average** (V08): how consistent each car's pace is, as the mean of its best X laps for X = 1…n;
- **lap overview** (V09): every lap of a car as a block, its heat within its stint, in/out laps, bests and the track status;
- **weather** (D26): the game's forecast for this session, with the first sample that brings rain;
- **crossover** (V19, D26): the field's lap times per tyre type, the 110 % line, the first car on another type faster than us, and the laps a switch takes to pay back.

Also in this run: the R20 follow-up of 4.1a (§7), and the screenshot baselines stay within the cap (§6.3).

Rules of every SP4 section hold (sections doc): positive = good for our car; each unit updates when its inputs change; missing data is `Known`, never 0; displays only render. Lap times are public: realism hides nothing here.

## 3. Units and topics

| Unit | Kind | Reads | Topic | Content |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `raising-average` | analysis, quick | `laps`, `lap-classes` | `raisingAverage.field` | per car the mean of its best X `forPace` laps, X = 1…n |
| `lap-overview` | analysis, quick | `laps`, `stints`, `session-phase-*`, `lap-classes` | `lapOverview.car` (every car) | per lap: time, stint, compound, in/out, best, heat, status |
| `rain-outlook` | calculator, quick | live session (forecast) | none | the first rain sample; rain within the horizon |
| `weather` | analysis, quick | live session (forecast), `rain-outlook` | `weather.forecast` | the samples, the accuracy flag, the first rain sample |
| `race-moment` (change) | analysis | `rain-outlook` replaces its forecast read | unchanged | the weather rule reads the calculator; behaviour unchanged |
| `crossover` | analysis, quick | `laps`, `pit-loss`, `session-phase-*`, `lap-classes` | `crossover.car` (`playerCar`, `teammateCar`) | field medians per tyre type, dry reference, 110 % line, our %, first faster, payback |

Every unit is quick: its inputs are bounded by the session, so C31's growth condition does not hold. Each reads the current version of each lap through `currentLaps` (C25). Cadence: the change tags of what it reads (`laps`, `stints`, the session-phase sub-machines, the live session area, `pit-loss`'s output, as each declares); no fixed tick, nothing here changes in between (A2).

Topic names: `raisingAverage.field` (not `.car`, as the carry-over had it), since the chart draws several cars in one panel and a panel's declared data is one subscription with fixed params; one value holds every car (20 cars × at most about 70 points). `crossover.car` (not `.field`), since "us", first faster and payback belong to one of our cars.

### 3.1 `raising-average`

- Laps: the session's `forPace` laps (`lap-classes`: valid, no in or out lap, neutralised time under the limit, not lap 1 of a race), raw lap times, all compounds together (B3). No fuel correction: the chart shows raw consistency.
- Per car: the `forPace` lap times sorted ascending; point X = the mean of the first X, for X = 1…n. A car with no `forPace` lap has an empty curve.
- Every car, rivals included.

### 3.2 `lap-overview`

Per car, per completed lap (every lap, in/out and invalid ones included):

- `lapTimeMs`, `lapNumber`, `stint` (index of the car's stint), `compound` (visual, from the lap record), `kind`: `in | out | normal`;
- `best`: `session` for the session's fastest `forBest.lap` lap, `personal` for the car's fastest, else `none`; as of now (a later faster lap moves the mark);
- `heat`: `Known<Ratio>`, 0 = the stint's fastest `forPace` lap, 1 = its slowest; `notApplicable` for a lap outside the stint's `forPace` laps, and for every lap of a stint with fewer than two;
- `status`: `green | vsc | sc | red` (B4). `sc` if the lap record's `scShare` > 0, else `vsc` if `vscShare` > 0; `red` if the lap's membership (C26, `membershipOf`) holds a session-phase `suspended` span, which wins over both. Recorded facts and the projection's membership only, no judging from times.

Behaviour table (C44) over the kinds of `session-phase-race`, `-qualifying`, `-practice`: `suspended` → `red`; every other kind → status by the shares.

### 3.3 `rain-outlook` and `weather`

`rain-outlook` (calculator, C43: the one judgement of "rain is coming", B6):

- `firstRain`: `Known<{ index, offsetMin, rainPct }>`, the first forecast sample with `offsetMin ≥ 0` and `rainPct ≥ rainPct`; `notApplicable` if none;
- `withinHorizon`: whether `firstRain.offsetMin ≤ horizonMin`.

Config moves from `race-moment` (`weatherRainPct`, `weatherHorizonMin`) to `rain-outlook` (`rainPct` 40 %, `horizonMin` 10 min, same sources). `race-moment`'s weather rule becomes "`rain-outlook` says rain within the horizon", with the same cause params (`rainPct`, `offsetMin`); its tests are ported (C82). A user `config.json` that still holds the old `race-moment` keys fails strict validation at start: the run's report says so, and the user moves them.

`weather` → `weather.forecast`: the session's forecast samples in time order as the live state has them (step 1 keeps this session's only), `forecastAccuracy`, and `firstRainIndex` from `rain-outlook` (`notApplicable` if none). The strip marks the first rain sample from this field; no rain band in the UI.

### 3.4 `crossover`

Tyre type of a lap: `slick` for a C-compound, `inter`, `wet` (the game's wet compounds, `classicWet` and `f2Wet` included) from `actualCompound`.

A **green lap** here: a `forPace` lap with `scShare` = `vscShare` = 0 and no `suspended` span in its membership. Behaviour table (C44) over the session-phase kinds: `suspended` and `neutralised` → excluded; every other kind → included.

Output for the param car (`playerCar` or `teammateCar`; a rival car gives `notApplicable`):

- `byLap`: per lap number, per tyre type, the median of the field's green laps of that type (`Known`, `insufficientData` below `minCars` laps);
- `dryRefMs`: the lowest slick median so far; `insufficientData` until one exists;
- `line110Ms`: `dryRefMs × line110`;
- `ourPct`: our latest green lap over `dryRefMs`, as a percentage;
- `firstFaster`: the first car (in completion order) on a type other than ours whose sector time on a green lap beats our car's same sector on the same lap number: car, type, sector, lap; `notApplicable` if none;
- `payback`: `Known<{ laps, lossSource }>` = green pit loss (`pit-loss`) / (our latest green lap − the fastest other type's latest median), `notApplicable` when no other type is faster; `lossSource` is `pit-loss`'s source (the badge).

## 5. Workbook `garage`

`longrun` is not a separate workbook (B1 C): its displays join `garage`, in two windows (B2 A):

- main (24"): calibration panel, stint scatter, raising average, lap overview, timing tower;
- side (14"): pit-loss table, crossover chart, forecast strip.

A starting point; the user tunes it. Defaults are seeded only when missing and never overwritten: an existing `PITWALL_DATA_DIR/ui/workbooks/garage.json` keeps its layout until the user deletes it (the run's report says so; nothing else is done).

## 6. Testing (`docs/testing.md`)

### 6.1 Units

- Builders for the edge cases: provisional then confirmed laps; a stint with fewer than two `forPace` laps; SC and VSC on one lap; a lap completed under suspension; our car alone on its type; no other type faster; a forecast without a rain sample, and one with rain beyond the horizon.
- Guarded views (C41) for all five units; behaviour-table completeness (C44) covers `lap-overview` and `crossover`.
- `race-moment`'s weather tests ported to `rain-outlook` (C82).
- Segments (C84): `lap-overview` on a VSC lap (`raceWetVscMexico`); `crossover` on `raceSingaporeWet`: the inter median falls below the slick median at some lap and `firstFaster` is set by then (behaviour, not the recording's numbers). The user checks the wet races live.

### 6.2 Displays

Row and emphasis tests per widget; gallery cases (`fit.spec` checks every case and theme); one baseline per display size of its first case.

## 7. R20: `battle.segments` speed

The slow part is running the 8 MB `race-zandvoort-full-lap` segment frame by frame (`SessionTracker`, `SessionRunner`: decoding, frame assembly, the four steps), not `battle`.

- Profile that path; fix the hot spots with identical output (determinism and acceptance guard it).
- Target: `battle.segments.test.ts` passes in at most 2 s alone (40 % of the 5 s timeout), so it holds under `pnpm check` load. No raised timeout, no shorter fixture.
- Guard: a work-count test where the hot spot has a countable measure (as `battle.work.test.ts`); a timer test only as the fallback, run alone.
- A fix in engine or protocol code changes the rules version and rebuilds stored sessions (C61, C90): expected. A fix in the engine core (C78) is made or delegated by name by the supervisor. If the cost is in the test harness (gunzip, building the frame list), the fix goes there.
