## 1. Intent

The user's weekend: "over time we establish driver and car and track profile data. In practice we finetune the numbers for that race. Before qualifying we do car tweaks, then in qualifying driver coaching. Once the grid is settled and we have our data we start the planning round: let the DP run, devise some human-made plans, compare and iteratively improve until we're happy. Then the race starts and we need to continuously update our strategy."

How real teams do it (research 06 §1.2): a few prepared plans, plus a standing answer for the SC that is kept current so the call takes seconds ("no live calculations are needed" on the pit wall). Pitwall's version, as the brainstorm settled it:

- **Plans are only stops.** A plan is a start compound (in the planning round) and its stops to the flag, nothing else. Plans differ by approach: A the DP's best, B one stop more or one less; optionally C (for example a team plan); and the game's own two strategies, entered by hand, to compare against what the Monte Carlo says of them.
- **Everything else is decided live**, from numbers computed in advance: an SC, a VSC or a weather change from the **decision card** (today's SC card: per condition SC, VSC and GREEN "box now", the six answers stay out / box S, M, H, I, W); the unexpected from the DP's live picks on the plan board; the instant from the **circle of doom**.
- **The planning round** in the menu after qualifying: pitwall prefills A and B, the engineer confirms or changes them and enters the game's plans; they carry into the race and stay fixed (a stable baseline, so a live pick that beats it by more than the noise is a real signal).
- **In the race** every plan is re-scored every lap; the DP shows its best plan per stop count beside them; replacing a plan or switching the active one is one click.

## 2. Goals, non-goals, anti-goals

**Goals**
- `Policy` becomes stops only; the trigger mechanism is deleted (§5.1).
- In the Monte Carlo, our car under a simulated SC or VSC makes the DP's best move for that state, without hindsight (§5.2).
- Plan slots per driver (A, B, C, G1, G2), one active, as recorded decisions shared by the team's feeds (C1a, C76).
- The plan board (V17), the decision card (V18 reworked), the strategy overview (V06), the circle of doom (V01, two panels), the `plan` and `strategy` workbooks.
- The planning round after qualifying with prefill and confirm, and the game's plans compared on the same numbers.

**Non-goals**
- Conditions on plans, trigger tracking on the live race, automatic switching. Pitwall never switches; the engineer does.
- No pit-entry marker and no rival ghosts on the circle.
- Planning before qualifying ends; carrying plans across weekends.
- Model work: pit loss by track pace (deferred item 10), the h1/h3 lap-time bias.
- Reading the game's strategies from telemetry: the UDP output carries only the player's ideal and latest pit lap and rejoin position (`m_pitStopWindowIdealLap`, `…LatestLap`, `m_pitStopRejoinPosition`), not the game's plans.

**Anti-goals**
- Scoring a plan with hindsight (a per-scenario best reaction).
- A second scoring of plans outside the strategy job's run (numbers that do not share the candidates' draws), or a second owner of the SC answer: the decision card, the MC's SC reaction and the DP's table are one judgement (C43).
- Any judgement in a display (C65); a circle that waits for a job; explaining text in displays.

## 4. Approaches (chosen in the brainstorm)

- **Plans and scoring: plans are decisions, scored by the strategy job in the same run as the DP's candidates (chosen).** Rejected: plans as UI state (C75 forbids UI state changing results within a session); a separate plans job (its numbers would not share the candidates' draws; a second owner, C43).
- **A, B, C relate: separately (chosen).** Each plan scored as followed; the board shows the numbers, the engineer decides. Rejected: one plan with branches; conditions on plans at all (user: conditions building on conditions get "way too complex way too quickly"; SC, VSC and weather are decided live).
- **Our car's SC reaction inside the Monte Carlo: the DP's best move for the state, no hindsight (D, chosen).** Rejected: a fixed rule of thumb like the rivals' (A: picks box/stay and compound by a rule, not by value); no reaction (B: every plan reads worse than the real race); the best reaction per scenario (C: hindsight, and a Monte Carlo inside the Monte Carlo).
- **Plan A and B: prefilled from the DP, confirmed by the engineer, then fixed (chosen).** Rejected: A and B as "whatever the DP says now" (they would flicker between tied plans every lap and leave no baseline).
- **The planning round: on the finished qualifying run's final view, in the bridge (chosen, §7).** Rejected: grid and formation only (user: planning "mostly happens while the driver is in the menu after the end of qualifying"); keeping the qualifying session open (no frames arrive); building the race's view from stored projections alone (the starting point needs calculator outputs, C50, which only a run has).

## 5. 4.3d1: the model change

### 5.1 Plans are stop lists
- `Policy` becomes `{ startCompound?: NoiseCompound; stops: { lap, compound }[] }`; `startCompound` only before the start (the planning round, §7.3). `Trigger` is deleted (domain, supervisor).
- Deleted with it (each in the task that moves its readers): `strategy-dp/thresholds.ts` and every trigger on its plans, `boxThisLap`'s and `continue`'s triggers; `race-monte-carlo`'s `playOut`, `rainBox`, `boxAt`, `rivalPits` and the trace run the cover rule needed (`cost: 2`); the rule candidates cover and extend (mirror ahead and behind stay); `McRow.triggers`; the strategy table's trigger count and hover.
- Old decisions sidecars hold `planCommitted` with triggers: the decoder accepts and drops them (supervisor's call: old recordings replay, their triggers ignored). The recorded `planCommitted` facts then carry a different `policy`, so the log changes: the rules version changes (C61, C90) and stored sessions are rebuilt once.

### 5.2 Our car's SC and VSC reaction (D)
- In every scenario of `race-monte-carlo`, when an SC or VSC starts, each of our cars that follows a plan makes **one choice, on the neutralisation's first lap**: the move `strategy-dp`'s adaptive table values best for its node (compound, sets used, found along the plan's earlier stops) and tyre age at that lap, from the values **averaged over the condition's length distribution** (`neutral.sc/vsc.{stay, box, boxArg}`), never from the scenario's own length (that would be hindsight). Stay out: the plan goes on (a planned stop inside the neutralisation stays where it is). Box onto the table's compound: after it the car follows the table's best continuation from that node and lap (a new `strategy-dp` export over its internal path walk, O(laps) per reaction), not the plan's later stops. Later laps of the same neutralisation bring no second choice (supervisor's call: the call is made when it is deployed, as on the pit wall).
- `MAX_STOPS` (3) counts stops from `fromLap`: a car that has used them gets no box move; accepted (supervisor's call).
- The table is the heaviest weather world's, as `boxThisLap` today (supervisor's call; the per-world table is the builder's if it fits the budget).
- The DP is solved once per run and only queried here; the reaction is part of `race-monte-carlo`'s scenario play, the one place (C43).
- Ownership (C43): the reaction is the simulator's model of what we would do, read from the DP (the one owner of "the best move for a state"); the decision card (§6.3) is the analysis that ranks the six answers by finishing position from its own Monte Carlo. They answer different questions (expected time from a state; positions against the field) and may rank differently; no test demands they agree (supervisor's call).
- Rivals keep their rules (`rivalScBoxChance`, `rivalScMinTyreAge`).

### 5.3 The strategy job with slots
- Candidates per car: the saved plans of its slots (source `plan`, with their slot), the DP's best plan per stop count 0–3 (as 4.3c), stop-lap neighbours, mirror candidates; the active plan first.
- Saved plans are exempt from the race's drop (`zDrop`) and always re-scored with the top set; `rescoreTop` counts the leaders, the slots come on top (supervisor's call: a slot's numbers must be as exact as the leaders', and the slots must not push a leader out). They spend from the same `maxScenarioPolicies` cap.
- `McRow` gains `slot: Slot | null`; `McSource` gains `'plan'` and `'game'`. Each row's paired difference and SE against the active plan (`diffPlan`, `sePlan`, places) are computed on the scenarios both were scored on: the re-scoring's for rows in the re-scored set (the active plan always is), the search's otherwise (supervisor's call).
- A saved plan that can no longer be followed is followed as far as it can be (a passed stop boxes this lap), as today, and shows `infeasible`.
- The board's live picks are the best row per stop count among non-slot rows (user: "the best with one more or one less stop").

## 6. 4.3d1: slots, the board, the decision card, the overview, the circle

### 6.1 Plan slots (decisions)
- **Slots** per our car, a closed list in `domain`: `A`, `B`, `C`, `G1`, `G2` (the game's two). All optional; the active plan is one of them.
- **Decisions** (domain): `planSaved { carId, slot, policy }`, `planRemoved { carId, slot }`; `planCommitted { carId, policy, slot? }` stays the active plan's decision (activating a slot commits its policy, slot named); `planCleared` stays. `latestPlan` unchanged; new `planSlots(history, carId)` (C25).
- **uiActions**: `plan.save`, `plan.remove`, `plan.activate`; `plan.commit`, `plan.clear` stay. `plan-routing` routes them to the team's feeds (C1a) with its range check (C73: laps within the race, compounds and slots from their closed lists).
- **A feed that joins the team later** is handed each car's newest decision per slot and its active plan (C76 changes, §9.1).
- Editing (a lap, a compound, add or drop a stop) is a window-local draft until saved (C73).

### 6.2 The plan board (V17), widget `plan-board`
- Reads `strategy.car` (live) or, in the planning round, `plans.preview` and `plans.prepared` (§7.4) by a panel option `source: live | planning` (one widget; supervisor's call).
- Per driver: the slots (active marked, ΔPOS and ΔTIME against it, positive = good, `±` the MC's SE, the finishing distribution strip, infeasible greyed), then the DP's best per stop count with "→ slot". In the planning round a START column (the start compound) and the race inputs in the header (grid place, laps entered, rain chance).
- Actions (declared uiActions, C74; commands, C72): save to a slot, edit, activate, remove; in the planning round accept the prefill, enter a game plan.
- Presentation table over `session-phase-race` (C66); cold-start numbers carry their source badge.

### 6.3 The decision card (V18 reworked): `sc-call` → per condition, six answers
- **Per condition** SC, VSC and GREEN ("if we box this lap"), every lap in advance: rows stay out, box S, M, H, I, W; per row E[pos], ΔPOS against staying out, P(gain), the rejoin position (from `rejoin`, C43), the finishing distribution; a compound without a set left shows "no set". Each box row is "box now onto X, then the DP's best continuation", scored with §5.2's reaction for later neutralisations.
- **With an SC or VSC out:** only that condition's block, its remaining length from `race-hazard`; GREEN hidden (supervisor's call: no green stop is possible).
- The widget stays `sc-card` (its name; supervisor's call to avoid a rename across workbooks), in the `strategy` workbook (user).
- Budget: ≤ 5 s per car on its own thread (Q9 of 4.3b); 3 conditions × up to 6 rows; the cap set from a measurement in the plan.

### 6.4 The strategy overview (V06): analysis `strategy-overview`, widget `strategy-overview`
- Per our car, the cars within one pit loss ahead or behind (user), the loss from `pit-loss` (C43), gaps from `gaps`; the teammate always. Per car: position, gap, compound, tyre age, stops, owes a compound, past stints, the predicted next stop window and next-compound odds (`rival-stops`); our cars' rows the active plan's stops and plan B's. Topic `strategyOverview.car`; behaviour table (C44); cadence per line crossing or faster where `rival-stops` changes.
- Widget: one row per car on a lap axis, past stints as blocks, a rival's window as an uncertain block, ours as the plan's stints.

### 6.5 The circle of doom (V01): analysis `circle`, widget `circle-of-doom`
- **The method is the track map's ghost (P25, P26).** `rejoin-ghost` already places the ghost at "where our car was `lossMs` ago", from our own car's crossing history (dense, always in the past: `mapPointAt` in `rejoin-ghost/calculator.ts`, interpolating between our crossings). The circle uses the inverse of the same history: **a car's place on the circle is how long ago our car was where that car is now** (its live lap position against our crossing times, interpolated, modulo one of our laps). A car just behind sits seconds behind us, a car ahead near the far side; a car that spins or stops slides back every frame. No change to `gaps`.
- **One helper, reused (user: reuse the code there).** The interpolation over our crossings moves out of `rejoin-ghost` into `analysis/src/shared/` as one pure helper with both directions: time → our position (the ghost) and position → the time we were there (the circle, the rejoin order). `rejoin-ghost` is moved onto it in the same task; no second copy (C43).
- **`rejoin` counts its rejoin position and crossing order the same way, every frame** (the one owner, C43): the cars between our car and the ghost on that scale, lapped cars excluded as today. Its readers (`crossing-order`, `rejoin.car`, the decision card's rejoin, the circle) get the live value. Its cadence moves from `ourCrossing` (every mini-sector crossing of our car) to every frame; budget in §8.
- **Analysis `circle`**, every frame (`maxHz` 10): per our car, every car's angle by the time above, our car at the top; our ghosts per condition at `rejoin`'s loss; per ghost the rejoin position and the time to the car ahead and behind on the same scale (from `rejoin`); the current condition. Topic `circle.car`.
- **Widget:** one circle per panel, two panels in `strategy` (user), cars with driver codes, our drivers marked, the current condition's ghost highlighted, the others faint, per ghost its rejoin position and two gaps. No pit-entry marker, no rival ghosts.
- **Label rule (P28):** labels of cars closer than a label's width on the circle fan out along the radius; in a group too tight to fan, the car closest to our car (or our teammate) keeps its code and the others show dots only, their codes on hover.

## 7. 4.3d2: the planning round

### 7.0 The gap between sessions (rules, each with a test)
1. **A session ends without waiting for the next packet.** The bridge ends a session when its SEND grace window (10 s) has passed, by a timer, not only on the next packet: the SEND frame is applied, `runner.finish` closes every span, storage finishes the session (C60) and its recording closes. The log is the one a later packet would have produced (C38: reprocess equals live).
2. **Between sessions the feed is not stale.** From a session's end until the next session's first packet, `feed.status` reports `betweenSessions` and no `feed.stale` insight is raised (C52's feed insights stay for silence inside a session).
3. **A plan uiAction between sessions is rejected** (no session runs); the planning round's uiActions (`plans.prepare.*`) are the only plan input then.
4. **The planning job reads the stored qualifying session, as of its end** (the as-of view `pitwall score` uses: `calc/as-of.ts` `asOfStored`, `situationAtEnd`): the situations at its end, the projection, the session's priors, track file and profiles snapshots (its sidecars), and every calculator the job needs run once on demand against that view (C49). Nothing is kept in memory across the gap, so a bridge restart in the gap loses nothing: at startup the bridge finishes an incomplete session whose recording holds its SEND (rule 1 applied to the replay, C59, C61).
5. **Which qualifying:** the newest qualifying-phase session of the weekend whose player car has our team and race number (C62), restarts included (the newest attempt). Long format Q1/Q2/Q3: the grid is Q3's order for its cars, then Q2's eliminated cars by Q2 order, then Q1's by Q1 order (from `view.weekend`, C62). A sprint shootout plans the sprint; qualifying plans the grand prix.
6. **Prepared plans are keyed by the race they are for:** `plans/<weekendId>/<format>/<team>.json`, `format` the race's weekend-phase format (`grandPrix`, `sprint`, the short variants), so a sprint weekend's two planning rounds never collide.
7. **The race-start commit:** at the race's first frame with its weekend phase decided (race, with a format matching the file) **and** its team known, the team's first feed in the race commits each car's prepared slots and active plan, for both cars, to every team feed then running (C76); the bridge notes them as the team's newest plans (`notePlan`), so a feed that joins later is handed them per slot (§9.1), never the file again. The commit skips the lap range check: a stop past the race's laps shows its row `infeasible` (rule from §7.1 step 8). The file is read once per race session.
8. **A race restarted by the league** (a new session uid, same weekend and format, before any lap is completed) is a new race session: rule 7 commits the prepared plans again (supervisor's call: the file holds what the engineer prepared; grid edits of the first attempt are lost, as every fact of it is).
9. **A flashback** voids decisions after its target (C27a); the race-start commit lies before any target a flashback can reach, so it stays.

### 7.1 The flow
1. Qualifying ends; the bridge ends and stores the session (§7.0 rule 1).
2. The `plan` workbook shows the planning round (the boards' `source: planning`).
3. The engineer enters the race's laps once (the lobby setting); the bridge suggests the track's last stored race laps (supervisor's call).
4. The bridge runs the **planning job** on the stored qualifying session as of its end (§7.0 rule 4), through the job runner and its worker limit, triggered by the planning uiActions instead of the scheduler (a new trigger path in the bridge, supervisor's call): the race's starting point (§7.2), the DP per driver, the Monte Carlo over the DP's best per stop count, the neighbours, the prepared slots and the game's plans.
5. **Prefill:** with no prepared plans yet, A = the best row, B = the best row with one stop more or one less (the better of the two). The engineer accepts, changes, adds C, enters G1 and G2 (the game's plans, start compound and stops) and picks the active one. Every change re-runs the job (seconds).
6. The race loads: the bridge commits the prepared plans as §7.0 rule 7 says. From then 4.3d1 applies.
7. If the race loads before the engineer is done, what is prepared then carries in. Without any prepared plans, the race's board prefills on the grid the same way, waiting for a confirm (supervisor's call).
8. The race's laps differ from the entered ones: the committed plans keep their stops; a stop past the flag shows the row `infeasible`; the board on the grid offers the new prefill (supervisor's call).

### 7.2 The race's starting point from the qualifying session
- `forecast-field` gains a second entry, the grid start (one shared builder, C50): the grid (the qualifying projection's last order; cars without a time at the back), fuel for the entered laps, fresh tyres; our cars' start compound free (the DP chooses it, the plan carries it as `startCompound`); rivals' start compounds drawn from the weekend's sets and the track file's defaults; the race's weather from qualifying's race forecast samples (§7.3); the hazard from the track file; priors (the set the race would preselect, C62a) and profiles (C62c) as the run holds them; the calculators' outputs on the as-of view (§7.0 rule 4).
- One planning job per feed, each for its own player car with the teammate on its prepared active plan, as the race's strategy job (supervisor's call; the store is per team, each feed's job reads it). Priors, track and profiles are the qualifying session's snapshots (its sidecars): the race of the same weekend and track would be handed the same preselected set, track file and profiles (supervisor's call; a calibration edit between qualifying and race reaches the race through its own session start, C62a).
- Units the starting point needs must give values on the qualifying as-of view (their behaviour tables, C44). Today (gap survey): `tyre-life`, `tyre-model`, `pit-loss`, `degradation` compute (pit-loss and degradation as non-race); **`driver-noise`, `rival-stops`, `track-wetness`, `race-hazard` and `forecast-field`'s builder answer `notApplicable` outside the race**: each gains a qualifying row for the planning job (driver-noise from the session's laps and the profiles, rival-stops on its priors with no stop seen, race-hazard from the track file, track-wetness from the race forecast), and the builder's grid-start entry is the only path that builds outside the race.
- `strategy-dp` gains a free start compound.
- What is unknown is `unknown(reason)` (C3) and shown.

### 7.3 Keeping the race forecast
Step 1 keeps, as values, the forecast samples of every session the packet lists, by session (step 1 may read `sessionType` to shape them, C8; no signal enters the values, C13). The qualifying session's live state holds the race's forecast at its end, and the as-of view reads it from the session's recording replayed to its end (§7.0 rule 4); no new fact. This changes the rules version (C61).

### 7.4 Where it lives
- Prepared plans: bridge user state in `PITWALL_DATA_DIR/plans/<weekendId>/<team>.json` (per car its slots, the active slot, the entered laps), changed only by `plans.prepare.*` uiActions (C70, C73), published as the core topic `plans.prepared`.
- The planning job's result: the core topic `plans.preview`, published by the bridge's planning module (C55's bridge-state rule), a job result outside the determinism hash.
