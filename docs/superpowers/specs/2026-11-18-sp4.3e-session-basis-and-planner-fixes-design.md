# SP4.3e: session basis and planner fixes (design)

Status: revision 2, for the user's approval, 2026-11-18. Revision 1 (a declared "continued weekend", approved and applied to the contract in af2978ca and 7a655458) is replaced by the session basis (user, 2026-11-18: a standing feature, chosen per session, "none" allowed); its contract wording is replaced with §4.1's on approval. Input: the Texas live test of 2026-11-17 (`docs/acceptance/live-checklist.md`, 2026-11-17 sections) and the research report `~/dev/personal/pitwall-research/live-test-2026-11-17/planner-report.md` (root causes verified in code, storage and recording bytes).

## 1. Intent

The league ran a full Texas weekend on 2026-11-17 (1 h practice, short qualifying), then the EA servers crashed before the race. The race (56 laps, grand prix) is driven on 2026-11-18 in a **new lobby**, with **no qualifying** (the grid is set by hand to the 2026-11-17 qualifying order) and at most a short practice. The drivers practised a lot already; they must not have to do another full practice.

The user wants this as a **standing feature**, not a one-off: lobbies change often (crashes, warm-up lobbies, LAN setups), and a weekend split over evenings or lobbies is normal in the league. So:

1. **Every session declares what it builds on, its basis** (user, 2026-11-18): at the start of a qualifying, its practice; at the start of a race or sprint, its practice and its qualifying. The basis is chosen per session, whatever weekend id or lobby the earlier sessions had, so no lobby in between can take it. **"None" is a valid basis**: a wet race after a dry practice is better served by no data than by wrong data (user, 2026-11-18), and every unit copes with it.
2. **The planner's output is right**: the planning round on 2026-11-17's qualifying gave wrong grids, `insufficientData`, an inter plan in the dry and a missed better plan. Its root causes are fixed properly, not patched.
3. **It is verified before the race** (§6).

Driver profiles and the calibration set already carry over (they are per person and per track) and stay as they are.

## 2. Goals, non-goals, anti-goals

**Goals**
- G1. Each session has a basis: for a qualifying (or sprint shootout) a list of practice sessions; for a race or sprint a list of practice sessions and at most one qualifying (shootout); or none. The basis is the session's weekend context (`view.weekend`, C39, C62).
- G2. At session start the basis is preselected (§4.2) and shown in the top bar; the user confirms or changes it at any time in the session, including "none".
- G3. The planning round belongs to a qualifying session; it can be opened on any stored ended qualifying; a race commits, at its first frame, the prepared plans of the qualifying in its basis.
- G4. With a basis of none (or a thin one), nothing fails or guesses: pre-race pace is a declared stand-in or `insufficientData`, there is no planning round, and the live strategy builds up from the race's own laps.
- G5. The planner's root causes 1–5 and 6g of the report are fixed (§4.4).
- G6. A readiness check (§6) shows, before the race, that the grid, every car's pace, the sets, the candidates and the calibration values are plausible.
- G7. Tonight's temporary changes are reverted (§7).

**Non-goals**
- Bases across tracks (a session's basis is always on its own track).
- Pace per car as a track prior in the calibration set: later, if at all.
- The degradation priors' full remodel (report §5, fixes 2–4: race-length compatibility, uncertainty flag, first-lap effect): a later spec with validation on races (memory: SP4.3 enhanced care). Only fix 1 (paired offsets) is in scope, §4.4.
- UI items 6c (link-group pin), 6d (person names), 6e (calibration edit off the main thread), 6f (manual stint exclusion): next sub-project, §8.

**Anti-goals** (a failure even if it works)
- A basis that a replay or reprocess does not reproduce (C38), or that changes without being visible.
- Any fix that patches a reader instead of the owner of a judgement (C43; memory: structural fixes, not workarounds).
- A grid, pace or plan that silently uses a 0 or a made-up value (C3): every stand-in is declared and marked; "none" shows as missing data, never as default numbers presented as measured.

## 3. Approaches

- **A. A continued weekend** (revision 1): the user declares before a weekend that it continues weekend W. Replaced: it binds to "the next weekend", so a warm-up lobby can take it, and it cannot express "none" for one session.
- **B. Automatic continuation** (same track, same seats, within N hours): rejected (fires when unwanted, invisible).
- **C. A session basis (picked, user 2026-11-18):** per session, the recordings it builds on, preselected and visible, changed by a recorded decision, "none" allowed. It is the same shape as the calibration choice (C62a: a session-start snapshot, changed by a decision), so it reuses that pattern.

## 4. Design

### 4.1 The contract change (C0: first, with the ADR line, before any code)

On approval, revision 1's wording in C62, C62d, C75, C38, C59 and the glossary is replaced by this (ADR 0015 gets the reasons):

- **Glossary, new term:** "basis: the stored sessions a session builds on (C62): for a qualifying its practice sessions; for a race its practice sessions and its qualifying; for a sprint its practice sessions and its shootout; or none." The term "continued weekend" is removed.
- **C62, replacing its second half and revision 1's addition:** "A session's weekend context is its basis. At session start, once its team is known, the bridge preselects the basis: the session's own weekend's earlier sessions of the player's seat (those whose player car has the player car's team and race number); without any, per kind of the basis the newest stored sessions on the same track whose player car has that team and race number, however old; else none. It writes the preselection to the basis sidecar next to the recording. The user changes the basis at any time in the session by a basis-chosen decision (C76) that names the sessions or none; a choice on one feed is committed on each of the team's feeds (C1a). `view.weekend` gives the projections of the basis named by the newest basis-chosen decision in the projection, else of the session-start basis, each with the team and race number of every car slot; a recording without a basis sidecar has the preselection's first rule as its basis. A unit that reads `view.weekend` declares `decisions` in its `reads`; no accumulator holds it (C31). `[type, test]`"
- **C62d, replacing "Between a weekend's qualifying and its race" and the commit sentence, and revision 1's addition:** "The planning round belongs to an ended qualifying session (or shootout). The bridge opens it at that session's end and, on a uiAction, on any stored ended qualifying whenever no session runs on the feed (it replays the recording to its end, as the startup restore does). … The prepared plans belong to the team and the qualifying they were prepared on; the bridge stores them in `PITWALL_DATA_DIR/plans/`. At a race's first frame with its weekend phase decided and its team known, the bridge commits the prepared plans of the qualifying in the race's session-start basis as plan decisions on each of the team's feeds then running (C76); a basis chosen later commits none."
- **C39:** "`weekend` (the projections of the session's basis, each with the team and race number of every car slot, C62)".
- **C75:** "a basis change takes effect only through a basis-chosen decision (C62)" replaces revision 1's clause.
- **C76:** "Choosing a basis is a decision: it names the sessions (or none), never their data."
- **C38, C59:** the sidecar lists say "basis" for revision 1's "weekend" / "weekend continuation".

### 4.2 The basis in the bridge

- **Domain types (supervisor, C78):** `SessionBasis = { practice: SessionRef[]; qualifying: SessionRef | null } | null` (`null`: none); the decision kind `basisChosen { basis: SessionBasis }`; the sidecar `{ start: SessionBasis; rule: 'ownWeekend' | 'sameSeatRecent' | 'none' }` at `<recording>.basis.json`.
- **Preselection** (`apps/bridge/src/pipeline/session-basis.ts`, new): at the first Participants (where the team is first known and the weekend context is loaded today, `SessionProcessor.#contextFor`, `session-processor.ts:339`), per the C62 rule: own weekend; else per kind (practice: every practice session of the newest practice weekend on the track and seat; qualifying: the newest ended qualifying or shootout on the track and seat, for a race or sprint only) however old (user, 2026-11-18: no age limit; the top bar shows the dates); else none. Practice and qualifying are picked independently (my call: the newest practice and the newest qualifying need not share a weekend id, which is exactly the crash case). Written to the sidecar at that moment.
- **Loading:** `SessionStorage.weekendContext` loads the projections of the basis's sessions instead of a weekend id's (a `WeekendRepository` method `contextOf(sessions: SessionKey[], player)`; stale ones rebuilt in the background as today). The TEMP alias hack is deleted.
- **Changing:** a uiAction `basis.choose { feedId, basis: SessionBasis }` is committed as a `basisChosen` decision on each of the team's feeds (as plan decisions, C1a, C76). The engine's `view.weekend` follows the newest one (like `view.priors`, C62a). Units that read `view.weekend` (`weekend-pace`, `gridOf`'s later segments, and the other readers found by grep) declare `decisions` in their reads.
- **Showing:** a `basis.active` core topic per feed (C54: the session's basis, its rule, and per session its date, kind and track), the top bar shows "builds on: Practice 17 Nov · Qualifying 17 Nov" (or "no basis"), and a shell command "Basis: …" opens a picker of the candidate sessions on the track (the newest first) with "none" (C72). The CLI gets `pitwall basis [--feed id]` (show) and `pitwall basis choose <sessionRef…|none>`.
- **Replay and reprocess** read the sidecar and the decisions (C38).

### 4.3 The planning round on a qualifying

- **Key:** `RaceKey` becomes `{ qualifying: SessionRef-without-feed (sessionUid + weekendId); team }`, the file `plans/<weekendId>/<qualifyingSessionUid>/<team>.json` (the format follows from the qualifying's phase). The existing file `plans/548397152/grandPrix/0.json` is moved by a one-off migration step of the store on first read (my call; tonight's plans are not needed, but the migration keeps the store consistent).
- **Opening on demand:** a uiAction `plans.round.open { feedId, qualifying: SessionRef }` and a command "Planning: open round on <qualifying>": when no session runs on the feed, the pipeline replays that recording to its end (a new `FeedPipeline.restoreEndedNow(row)`: the startup restore's path, usable after start while between sessions, with packets that arrive meanwhile buffered as the restart restore does) and opens its round.
- **Commit:** `FeedPipeline.#raceStart` passes the race's session-start basis qualifying; `Planning.raceStarted` reads the prepared plans of that qualifying. A race without a qualifying in its basis commits nothing.
- **Fresh race sets:** a new lobby gives a full allocation in its first session, whether that is a qualifying or a race (user, 2026-11-18); the qualifying's view cannot know where the race will run. The planning round therefore takes a per-round option `raceSets: 'carried' | 'fresh'`, passed to `planningInputFrom` → `buildGridStart`: `carried` (the qualifying's sets plus the race-reserved ones) when the round opens at the qualifying's end, `fresh` (`freshRaceSets`, §4.4 item 3) when it is opened later on a stored qualifying (my call: a reopened round is the break-or-crash case); the round's board shows the option and lets the user switch it.

### 4.4 Planner fixes (no contract change; report §1–§4, 6g)

Each item: owner module, what changes, the report's tests. Every recorder or sub-machine change changes the rules version (C61, C90): stored sessions rebuild.

1. **Laps recorder: a lap completes only at a crossing of the line** (report §1; `engine/src/recorders/laps.ts`, `lap-boundary.ts`). A rise of the lap number without a crossing (lap distance falls by more than half the track length, or from below 0 to 0 or more) appends no lap and marks the lap number not completed, so Session History confirms nothing for it either. Segment tests on both qualifying recordings around 1115–1121 s.
2. **`car-activity-timed`: the in-lap status at the line is a pending confirmation** (report §2; `situations/car-activity-timed/timed.ts`, C18). Declared transitions unchanged; the transition table rows split (status mid-lap / on the tick before the line); segment tests DR 214–219 s (car 18), ASY 443–448 s (car 9); `pitwall scan-situations` over every recording in the report (C85).
3. **Race sets in the race's starting point** (report F3a; `analysis/src/shared/forecast-field`, the one builder, C50): on a grid start the sets are those `available` now plus those with `recommendedSession` equal to the race's session type. **With the round's option `raceSets: 'fresh'` (§4.3; user, 2026-11-18: the new lobby gives every driver a fresh full allocation),** the builder plans with a fresh race allocation instead of the qualifying's sets: per car the dry compounds' counts of a race-only weekend's allocation (a config entry of the builder, `freshRaceSets`, C47, its defaults measured from the first TyreSets of a race in a fresh lobby: the aborted race of 2026-11-18 00:37, recordings `2026-11-18_00-37-17_6ca2663a73e7b608_car18/19.pwrec.gz`). The live strategy reads the race's real sets from its first TyreSets as today; the readiness check (§6) compares the plans' sets with the real ones at tomorrow's race load. The fitted-set question (does a used qualifying set count as a start option) is settled by the builder as: the race's free start may use any set the race has (used ones included) and the DP's free start keeps taking a new set unless only used ones exist; marked as the builder's call in the plan.
4. **The rain filter is never bypassed; one owner** (report F3b, F3c): `bestPlanOf` and `candidatesOf` apply `allowed` to every fallback; with nothing allowed the car's entry is infeasible (C3). `rainCanCome`/`allowedOf` move to one shared pure function (or calculator) read by `strategy`, `pit-window` and the Box call (C43).
5. **One set-feasibility rule and the planning cap** (report F4b, F4c): one shared pure function "the sets a policy uses from a starting point" for the DP and `followable`; `partFinalCall` takes the cap from the chosen MC config (6,000 in the planning round, R34).
6. **Degradation α offsets as paired differences** (report §5 fix 1; `degradation/priors.ts`, its entry `version` changes, the priors store recomputes, C62a): per (session, car) the compound's α minus that car's medium α in the same session, then pooled; sources without a pair give ν but no offset. As a data step (no code): remove the 2026-11-04 race stints from `tx-1104` (its tyre life is half the race's, report §5c).
7. **A declared pace stand-in** (report 6g; in `weekend-pace`, the owner of pre-race pace, C43): a car with no long run and no valid qualifying lap takes the field's median qualifying pace plus a config offset (`noPaceStandInOffsetMs`, `config.ts`, C47), marked `standIn: true` in the output; the grid start reads it like any pace. Without any qualifying evidence in the field (a basis of none, or no qualifying in it) there is no stand-in: every car without its own evidence stays `unknown('insufficientData')` (G4), and the live strategy waits for the race's laps as today. Tonight's TEMP HACK in `forecast-field` is deleted.
8. **The planning-round save on the teammate's board** (report 6a): the two silent-ok `invoke` defaults (`kit/commands.ts`, `Panel.svelte`) answer `notSupported`; the teammate board's panel gets the registry's `invoke`. (C72, C73.)

`minLongRunLaps` stays 8 (the config default); the stand-in (7) and the in-lap fix (2) make the lower value unnecessary. To be checked in §6.

### 4.5 Order and tasks

Contracts first (C81): the contract and ADR change (§4.1); then the domain and contracts types (supervisor: `SessionBasis`, the `basisChosen` decision, the basis sidecar type, the uiActions `basis.choose` and `plans.round.open`, the `basis.active` topic, the `RaceKey` change, the `raceSets` option, the `standIn` source); then, two phases each (C82), with up to 8 parallel workers (memory: agent limit 8):

- T1 laps recorder (4.4.1) · T2 car-activity-timed (4.4.2) · T3 race sets + set rule + `raceSets` option (4.4.3, 4.4.5) · T4 rain filter owner (4.4.4) · T5 degradation paired offsets (4.4.6) · T6 pace stand-in (4.4.7) · T7a basis: preselection, sidecar, loader, decision, topic (4.2) · T7b planning round by qualifying, on-demand open, commit (4.3) · T7c basis UI: top bar, picker command, CLI (4.2) · T8 board save (4.4.8).

T1 and T2 share the rules-version rebuild; T3 and T5 both touch the planning round's inputs but different modules. Then `pnpm check`, a rebuild of stored sessions (once, at the end), `scan-situations` (T2), and §6.

## 5. Constraints

- The contract binds every change (C0); the contract change in §4.1 needs the user's approval with this design.
- Deadline: before the 2026-11-18 race, in the evening (user, 2026-11-18); the bridge may be stopped, rebuilt and restarted any time during the day. If time runs short, the order of value is: T7a and T7b (without them nothing carries over), T1, T2, T3, T6, T4, T5, T7c (the CLI can stand in for the UI), T8. Anything not done falls back to: the race without a planning round, the live strategy from lap 2–3.
- Compute: one reprocess at a time, `vitest --maxWorkers=3`, keep ≥ 6 GB free (memory). The rebuild of 236 stored sessions after T1/T2 takes long: run it once, at the end, with the machine otherwise idle.
- Tests on real data as segments only (C84); no test reads storage or the data dir.

## 6. Readiness check (before the race)

A script in the research repo (`live-test-2026-11-17/readiness.mjs`, read-only on storage and the bridge's topics) and a short checklist entry. After the rebuild, with the bridge running and the round reopened on 2026-11-17's qualifying (`plans.round.open`):

1. **Grid:** both feeds give the same grid, equal to the full-lap order (DR P1 1:35.365, Verstappen P2, … ASY P14 1:37.643, Hadjar P20).
2. **Pace:** every grid car has a `weekend-pace` value or a marked stand-in; no `insufficientData`.
3. **In-laps:** no AI flying lap of the qualifying is an in-lap on either feed.
4. **Sets:** the planning round plans with the fresh race allocation (`freshRaceSets`); at the race's load, the real TyreSets of both our cars match it (else the plans are re-checked before lights out).
5. **Candidates:** both our cars get several candidates per stop count, none with inters (rain 1 %); DR's DP list contains a medium-medium-soft 2-stop.
6. **Calibration:** `tx-1104` without the 11-04 race and with paired offsets: the hard offset clearly slower than medium (DR's sources alone gave hard +1570 ms and soft +167 ms against medium, report §5a; the paired rule should land near those); the values shown to the user before the race.
7. **Basis dry run:** a replay of a short race recording with a basis sidecar naming the 2026-11-17 practice and qualifying shows `view.weekend` with those sessions, and the commit of that qualifying's prepared plans; with basis none, `weekend-pace` is `insufficientData`/stand-in and nothing throws.
8. **The preselection for tomorrow:** at the race's first Participants the top bar shows the 2026-11-17 practice and qualifying (rule `sameSeatRecent`); the user confirms it before lights out.

A failing point is reported to the user with its effect on the race before the race starts.
