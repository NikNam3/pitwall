Status: revision 3, approved by the user 2026-11-18 (with the queue priority and the laps-shown-not-judged rule). Revision 1 (a declared "continued weekend", approved and applied to the contract in af2978ca and 7a655458) is replaced; its contract wording is replaced with §4.1's on approval. Input: the Texas live test of 2026-11-17 (`docs/acceptance/live-checklist.md`, 2026-11-17 sections) and the research report `~/dev/personal/pitwall-research/live-test-2026-11-17/planner-report.md` (root causes verified in code, storage and recording bytes).

## 1. Intent

The league ran a full Texas weekend on 2026-11-17 (1 h practice, short qualifying), then the EA servers crashed before the race. The race (56 laps, grand prix) is driven on 2026-11-18 in a **new lobby**, with **no qualifying** (the grid is set by hand to the 2026-11-17 qualifying order) and at most a short practice. The drivers must not have to do another full practice.

The user wants this as a **standing feature**: lobbies change often (crashes, warm-up lobbies, LAN setups), and a weekend split over days or lobbies is normal in the league. The workflow (user, 2026-11-18):

1. Qualifying ends; everyone goes to bed.
2. Some other day, the engineer opens pitwall with no session running, **picks the practice and qualifying** to plan on, and plans: slots and the active slot per car. They **save the plan** and may close pitwall (planning in the morning, racing in the afternoon).
3. When the race loads, the engineer **picks practice, qualifying and plan in the same picker** (preselected from the last choice) and confirms or changes them before lights out. **"None" is valid for each**: a wet race after a dry practice is better served by no data than by wrong data; with plan "none" the engineer plans then and there on the race's own board, as today.

And: the planner's output is right (the 2026-11-17 round gave wrong grids, `insufficientData`, an inter plan in the dry and a missed better plan; root causes fixed properly, not patched), verified before the race (§6).

Driver profiles and the calibration set already carry over (per person, per track) and stay as they are.

## 2. Goals, non-goals, anti-goals

**Goals**
- G1. **A basis**: the practice sessions (a list) and at most one qualifying (or sprint shootout) a session builds on, or none per kind; always on the session's track. It is the session's weekend context (`view.basis`, C39, C62).
- G2. **One picker** (practice, qualifying, plan), the same between sessions and in a session. Between sessions it chooses what the planning round plans on; in a session it chooses the session's basis and, for a race or sprint, its saved plan. Preselected (§4.2), shown in the top bar, changeable at any time.
- G3. **Saved plans**: a named, stored set of the team's slot choices per car (slots and active slot), not tied to any session or weekend; it notes what it was computed on, for display only. Saved, renamed and deleted by the engineer; survives closing pitwall.
- G4. **Choosing a saved plan in a race or sprint** commits its slots and active slot as plan decisions on each of the team's feeds. Choosing "none" commits nothing; the in-session board plans on the race's own view (`strategy.plans`, existing).
- G5. With a basis of none (or a thin one), nothing fails or guesses: pre-race pace is a declared stand-in or `insufficientData`, the round has nothing to plan on, and the live strategy builds up from the race's own laps.
- G6. The planner's root causes 1–5 and 6g of the report are fixed (§4.4).
- G7. A readiness check (§6) shows, before the race, that the grid, every car's pace, the sets, the candidates and the calibration values are plausible.
- G8. Tonight's temporary changes are reverted (§7).

**Non-goals**
- Bases across tracks.
- Pace per car as a track prior in the calibration set.
- The degradation priors' full remodel (report §5, fixes 2–4); only fix 1 (paired offsets) is in scope, §4.4.
- A saved plan adapted to a race it does not fit (laps, sets): the mismatch is shown, nothing is adjusted.
- UI items 6c (link-group pin), 6d (person names), 6e (calibration edit off the main thread), 6f (manual stint exclusion): next sub-project, §8.

**Anti-goals** (a failure even if it works)
- A basis or plan choice that a replay or reprocess does not reproduce (C38), or that changes without being visible.
- Any fix that patches a reader instead of the owner of a judgement (C43).
- A grid, pace or plan that silently uses a 0 or a made-up value (C3): every stand-in is declared and marked; "none" shows as missing data.
- Two pickers, or a plan bound to a weekend, format or qualifying key.

## 3. Approaches

- **A. A continued weekend** (revision 1): replaced; it binds to "the next weekend", a warm-up lobby can take it, and it cannot express "none".
- **B. Prepared plans keyed by the qualifying, committed at the race's first frame** (revision 2): replaced; it couples the plan to a basis choice made at race load (an override of the preselection loses the plans) and needs a second picker for the round.
- **C. One picker, saved plans as standalone things (picked, user 2026-11-18).** The basis and the plan are chosen together, by decisions in a session (the same pattern as the calibration choice, C62a), or as the round's input between sessions.

## 4. Design

### 4.0 The concepts (revision 3, after the coherence review, user 2026-11-18)

Five ideas carry the design; every part below is one of them, not a special case:

1. **A basis** is what a session (or a planning round) builds on: practice sessions and at most one qualifying, chosen per session, "none" allowed. It replaces the weekend context; its name says so (`view.basis`, `BasisContext`, C62 "Basis").
2. **One preselection rule**, per kind: the newer of the session's own weekend's earlier sessions and the team's last confirmed choice on the track; neither: none.
3. **Planning is a function**: plans = f(a qualifying recording, a practice basis, the race assumptions). A `PlanningRunner` replays the qualifying's recording in its own engine instance (as reprocess does), whether or not a game is running, at a qualifying's live end and the next morning alike.
4. **The race assumptions** `race: { laps, sets: 'carried' | 'fresh' }` are what the qualifying cannot know about the race to come; the user enters them on the board.
5. **Storage is a cache, stale is a cache miss**: one rebuild service `ensureCurrent(sessions)`, a queue in a worker thread, one at a time; whoever needs a session waits for it or does without. Nothing is rebuilt at bridge start, and no session's frames are ever held: a running session that needs a stale session goes on without it and gets it by a decision once current, like joining late.

Saved plans are templates of plan decisions: choosing one writes its slots as decisions; saving in a race reads them back.

### 4.2 The basis and the picker

- **Types (supervisor, C78):** `SessionRef = { sessionUid; weekendId }`; `SessionBasis = { practice: SessionRef[]; qualifying: SessionRef | null }` (empty and `null` are "none"); the decision `basisChosen { basis: SessionBasis }`; the sidecar `<recording>.basis.json` `{ start: SessionBasis; preselected: SessionBasis }` (`start`: the current sessions the session started with; `preselected`: for display only). `WeekendContext` → `BasisContext` (`packages/engine/src/state/`), `view.weekend` → `view.basis`, the four readers (`weekend-pace`, `shared/grid.ts`, `forecast-score/job.ts`, `track-wetness/dry-reference.ts`) renamed and declaring `decisions`.
- **The team's last confirmed choice** (bridge user state, `PITWALL_DATA_DIR/basis/<team>.json`): per track the last confirmed basis per kind and the last chosen or saved plan, each with its time.
- **Preselection** (`apps/bridge/src/pipeline/session-basis.ts`): at the first Participants (`SessionProcessor.#contextFor`), the C62 rule per kind; the session starts with its current sessions, the stale ones are queued with `ensureCurrent` and enter by a `basisChosen` decision once current (no frames held); both written to the sidecar. For the 2026-11-18 race: the qualifying kind takes the morning's choice (the new lobby has no qualifying); the practice kind too, unless a practice runs in the new lobby after the morning's choice; the plan is the morning's saved plan.
- **Loading:** `SessionStorage` loads the basis's sessions of the feed's seat through `WeekendRepository.contextOf(refs, player)`, after `ensureCurrent(refs)` (§4.3a). The background left-out-if-stale path (`session-storage.ts:147`) and the TEMP alias are deleted.
- **The picker** (one shell command "Basis and plan…", C72; the top bar shows "Practice 17 Nov · Qualifying 17 Nov · Plan: Texas 18 Nov 09:40", or "none" per part): practice (multi-select), qualifying (one), plan (one); candidates are the stored sessions on the track (newest first, dated, stale ones marked) and the team's saved plans on the track; "none" for each. A window-local draft until confirmed (C73). **On confirm:** `ensureCurrent` of the chosen sessions (progress shown), then, in a session, the `basisChosen` decision and the plan's decisions together on each of the team's feeds; between sessions, the last confirmed choice is updated and the planning round runs on it (§4.3).
- **Topic** `basis.active` per feed (C54): the basis, its session-start value, per session date, kind and track, the plan's name, the rebuild progress, and the team's last confirmed choice.
- **CLI:** `pitwall basis [--feed id]`, `pitwall basis choose --practice <ref…|none> --qualifying <ref|none> [--plan <name|none>]`.
- **Replay and reprocess** read the sidecar and the decisions (C38).

### 4.3 The planning round, saved plans and the rebuild service

- **`PlanningRunner`** (`apps/bridge/src/planning/runner.ts`, new): `open({ qualifying: SessionRef; practice: SessionRef[]; race: { laps; sets } })` replays the qualifying's recording (of the opening feed's seat) in its own `SessionRunner` to its end, in the rebuild service's worker (never on the thread that applies frames, so a running race does not stall; e.g. re-planning under a safety car), with the practice basis as `view.basis` (loaded after `ensureCurrent`) in place of the recording's basis sidecar and its `basisChosen` decisions, builds the as-of view (`asOfEnded`), and triggers the planning job; it publishes `plans.preview` and `plans.prepared` (the round's state: basis, race assumptions, slots, the team's saved plans on the track). At a live qualifying end, the bridge opens it on that qualifying (the replay costs ~30 s, measured 27 s, R33). **Deleted:** the pipeline's ended view kept for planning (`FeedPipeline.endedAsOf`, `#ended`), the startup restore of ended qualifyings (`cli/startup-restore.ts`, `restoreEndedSessions`, `endedQualifyingsOf`, `FeedPipeline.restoreEnded`, `ended-qualifying.ts`; the continuation of a running session's recording after a restart, C59, stays), the round per pipeline state, and their tests (C82: a test of a deleted mechanism is deleted with it; the round's behaviour tests are ported to the runner).
- **The race assumptions** `race: { laps; sets: 'carried' | 'fresh' }`: entered on the board (C73: one uiAction per field, validated); defaults: laps suggested as today, sets `carried` when the round opens at its qualifying's live end, else `fresh`. Passed to `planningInputFrom` → `buildGridStart` (§4.4 item 3).
- **Saved plans** (`planning/store.ts`, reworked): `SavedPlan = { id; name; team; trackId; savedAt; computedOn: { basis; race }; cars: PreparedCar[] }`, one file per plan, `plans/<team>/<id>.json`; uiActions `plans.save { feedId, name?, replaces? }` (from the round, or from the race's board), `plans.rename`, `plans.delete`. `RaceKey`, `sameKey`, the race-key commit and `plans/548397152/` are deleted.
- **Choosing a plan in a race:** at the first frame with the weekend phase decided and the team known, the preselected plan's slots are committed as `planSaved` per slot and `planCommitted` for the active slot, by race number (`#raceStart`, reworked); a plan chosen later commits its slots and removes the slots it lacks (`planRemoved`); "none" commits nothing. A plan whose stops lie past the flag or that uses a set the race does not have is infeasible, marked by `strategy`'s followable check (§4.4 item 5, C43); a plan computed for other race laps is information, not a judgement: `basis.active` carries the chosen plan's `computedOn.race.laps` and the race's laps, and the board shows both side by side (C65). Never adjusted.
- **3a. The rebuild service** (`apps/bridge/src/storage/rebuild-service.ts`, new; replaces `StaleSessions` scheduling in `cli/rebuild-database.ts` and the startup rebuild in `cli/serve.ts`): `ensureCurrent(refs: SessionKey[], onProgress?, priority?): Promise<void>`, a queue in a worker thread, one session at a time, by priority (user, 2026-11-18): first a confirmed basis of a running session, then planning replays (`PlanningRunner`), then everything else (first reads); a rebuild in progress finishes its session, then the highest priority goes next. Callers: the picker's confirm (waits, progress; in a running session the session goes on meanwhile and the choice enters by decision once current); a session's start with a stale preselection (the session starts with the current ones, the rest by decision once current, no frames held); the `PlanningRunner` (its replay runs in the same worker); other readers at their first read (calibration sources, profiles, `pitwall` CLI queries). Nothing at bridge start. A recording replayed to its end with no session running on it is complete (`complete` in storage).
  - **One writer, short transactions** (the storage is `better-sqlite3`, synchronous, one file): the worker only replays (the CPU part; the job runner's worker, `jobs/worker-entry.ts`, is the pattern) and sends the rebuilt log back in parts (structured clone); the main thread's storage writer is the database's only writer and writes the parts in short transactions between frames into a staging copy of the session's rows, then swaps the session's log in one short transaction (C24: the cached log is replaced as a whole). So the live feed never waits on a lock or a long transaction. A budget test (`*.budget.test.ts`): while a 1 h practice is rebuilt, the live pipeline's frame-apply latency stays within its current budget.
  - **Readers of a basis that changes in a session** (T7a): `view.weekend` was fixed for the session; each of the four readers is checked to hold nothing derived from it across frames (C31), with a test that its output follows a `basisChosen` decision at that frame.

### 4.4 Planner fixes (no contract change; report §1–§4, 6g)

Each item: owner module, what changes, the report's tests. Every recorder or sub-machine change changes the rules version (C61, C90).

1. **Laps recorder: a lap completes only at a crossing of the line** (report §1; `engine/src/recorders/laps.ts`, `lap-boundary.ts`). A rise of the lap number without a crossing (lap distance falls by more than half the track length, or from below 0 to 0 or more) appends no lap and marks the lap number not completed, so Session History confirms nothing for it either. Segment tests on both qualifying recordings around 1115–1121 s.
2. **`car-activity-timed`: the in-lap status at the line is a pending confirmation** (report §2; `situations/car-activity-timed/timed.ts`, C18). Declared transitions unchanged; the transition table rows split (status mid-lap / on the tick before the line); segment tests DR 214–219 s (car 18), ASY 443–448 s (car 9); `pitwall scan-situations` over every recording in the report (C85).
3. **Race sets in the race's starting point** (report F3a; `analysis/src/shared/forecast-field`, the one builder, C50): on a grid start the sets are those `available` now plus those with `recommendedSession` equal to the race's session type. With the race assumptions' `sets: 'fresh'` (§4.3) the builder plans with a fresh race allocation instead of the qualifying's sets: per car the dry compounds' counts of a race-only weekend's allocation (`freshRaceSets` in `forecast-field/config.ts`, C47, its defaults measured from the first TyreSets of the aborted race of 2026-11-18 00:37, recordings `2026-11-18_00-37-17_6ca2663a73e7b608_car18/19.pwrec.gz`). In a race the builder always reads the race's real sets. The fitted-set question is settled by the builder: the race's free start may use any set the race has (used ones included) and the DP's free start keeps taking a new set unless only used ones exist; marked as the builder's call in the plan.
4. **The rain filter is never bypassed; one owner** (report F3b, F3c): `bestPlanOf` and `candidatesOf` apply `allowed` to every fallback; with nothing allowed the car's entry is infeasible (C3). `rainCanCome`/`allowedOf` move to one shared pure function (or calculator) read by `strategy`, `pit-window` and the Box call (C43).
5. **One set-feasibility rule and the planning cap** (report F4b, F4c): one shared pure function "the sets a policy uses from a starting point" for the DP and `followable` (it also marks a chosen saved plan that uses a set the race lacks, §4.3); `partFinalCall` takes the cap from the chosen MC config (6,000 in the planning round, R34).
6. **Degradation α offsets as paired differences** (report §5 fix 1; `degradation/priors.ts`, its entry `version` changes, the priors store recomputes, C62a): per (session, car) the compound's α minus that car's medium α in the same session, then pooled; sources without a pair give ν but no offset. As a data step (no code): remove the 2026-11-04 race stints from `tx-1104` (report §5c).
7. **A declared pace stand-in** (report 6g; in `weekend-pace`, the owner of pre-race pace, C43): a car with no long run and no valid qualifying lap takes the field's median qualifying pace plus a config offset (`noPaceStandInOffsetMs`, `config.ts`, C47), marked `standIn: true` in the output; the grid start reads it like any pace. Without any qualifying evidence in the field (a basis without a qualifying) there is no stand-in: every car without its own evidence stays `unknown('insufficientData')` (G5). Tonight's TEMP HACK in `forecast-field` is deleted.
8. **The planning-round save on the teammate's board** (report 6a): the two silent-ok `invoke` defaults (`kit/commands.ts`, `Panel.svelte`) answer `notSupported`; the teammate board's panel gets the registry's `invoke`. (C72, C73.)

`minLongRunLaps` stays 8; to be checked in §6.

## 5. Constraints

- The contract binds every change (C0); the contract change in §4.1 needs the user's approval with this design.
- Deadline: before the 2026-11-18 race, in the evening. The waves put the critical path first (T7d → T7a-2, T7b → T7c-2, ★ in §4.5); a task that slips is the one the other waves do not need (T4, T5, T8, then T7c-2: the CLI stands in for the picker). - Tests on real data as segments only (C84); no test reads storage or the data dir.

## 6. Readiness check (before the race)

A script in the research repo (`live-test-2026-11-17/readiness.mjs`, read-only on storage and the bridge's topics) and a short checklist entry. After the rebuild, with the bridge running, the picker set to the 2026-11-17 practice and qualifying, and the round opened (`plans.round.open`, sets `fresh`):

1. **Grid:** both feeds give the same grid, equal to the full-lap order (DR P1 1:35.365, Verstappen P2, … ASY P14 1:37.643, Hadjar P20).
2. **Pace:** every grid car has a `weekend-pace` value or a marked stand-in; no `insufficientData`.
