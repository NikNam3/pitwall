# SP4.3e: session basis, saved plans and planner fixes (design)

Status: revision 3 (with the loading and joining rules, user 2026-11-18), for the user's approval, 2026-11-18. Revision 1 (a declared "continued weekend", approved and applied to the contract in af2978ca and 7a655458) is replaced; its contract wording is replaced with §4.1's on approval. Revision 2 (a per-session basis, prepared plans keyed by qualifying, committed at the race's first frame) is replaced by this revision after the user's review (2026-11-18): one picker for practice, qualifying and plan; a plan is a saved thing of its own, not tied to a session; "none" allowed for each. Input: the Texas live test of 2026-11-17 (`docs/acceptance/live-checklist.md`, 2026-11-17 sections) and the research report `~/dev/personal/pitwall-research/live-test-2026-11-17/planner-report.md` (root causes verified in code, storage and recording bytes).

## 1. Intent

The league ran a full Texas weekend on 2026-11-17 (1 h practice, short qualifying), then the EA servers crashed before the race. The race (56 laps, grand prix) is driven on 2026-11-18 in a **new lobby**, with **no qualifying** (the grid is set by hand to the 2026-11-17 qualifying order) and at most a short practice. The drivers must not have to do another full practice.

The user wants this as a **standing feature**: lobbies change often (crashes, warm-up lobbies, LAN setups), and a weekend split over days or lobbies is normal in the league. The workflow (user, 2026-11-18):

1. Qualifying ends; everyone goes to bed.
2. Some other day, the engineer opens pitwall with no session running, **picks the practice and qualifying** to plan on, and plans: slots and the active slot per car. They **save the plan** and may close pitwall (planning in the morning, racing in the afternoon).
3. When the race loads, the engineer **picks practice, qualifying and plan in the same picker** (preselected from the last choice) and confirms or changes them before lights out. **"None" is valid for each**: a wet race after a dry practice is better served by no data than by wrong data; with plan "none" the engineer plans then and there on the race's own board, as today.

And: the planner's output is right (the 2026-11-17 round gave wrong grids, `insufficientData`, an inter plan in the dry and a missed better plan; root causes fixed properly, not patched), verified before the race (§6).

Driver profiles and the calibration set already carry over (per person, per track) and stay as they are.

## 2. Goals, non-goals, anti-goals

**Goals**
- G1. **A basis**: the practice sessions (a list) and at most one qualifying (or sprint shootout) a session builds on, or none per kind; always on the session's track. It is the session's weekend context (`view.weekend`, C39, C62).
- G2. **One picker** (practice, qualifying, plan), the same between sessions and in a session. Between sessions it chooses what the planning round plans on; in a session it chooses the session's basis and, for a race or sprint, its saved plan. Preselected (§4.2), shown in the top bar, changeable at any time.
- G3. **Saved plans**: a named, stored set of the team's slot choices per car (slots and active slot), not tied to any session or weekend; it notes what it was computed on, for display only. Saved, renamed and deleted by the engineer; survives closing pitwall.
- G4. **Choosing a saved plan in a race or sprint** commits its slots and active slot as plan decisions on each of the team's feeds. Choosing "none" commits nothing; the in-session board plans on the race's own view (`strategy.plans`, existing).
- G5. With a basis of none (or a thin one), nothing fails or guesses: pre-race pace is a declared stand-in or `insufficientData`, the round has nothing to plan on, and the live strategy builds up from the race's own laps.
- G6. The planner's root causes 1–5 and 6g of the report are fixed (§4.4).
- G7. A readiness check (§6) shows, before the race, that the grid, every car's pace, the sets, the candidates and the calibration values are plausible.
- G8. Tonight's temporary changes are reverted (§7).

**Non-goals**
- Bases across tracks.
- Pace per car as a track prior in the calibration set.
- The degradation priors' full remodel (report §5, fixes 2–4); only fix 1 (paired offsets) is in scope, §4.4.
- A saved plan adapted to a race it does not fit (laps, sets): the mismatch is shown, nothing is adjusted.
- UI items 6c (link-group pin), 6d (person names), 6e (calibration edit off the main thread), 6f (manual stint exclusion): next sub-project, §8.

**Anti-goals** (a failure even if it works)
- A basis or plan choice that a replay or reprocess does not reproduce (C38), or that changes without being visible.
- Any fix that patches a reader instead of the owner of a judgement (C43).
- A grid, pace or plan that silently uses a 0 or a made-up value (C3): every stand-in is declared and marked; "none" shows as missing data.
- Two pickers, or a plan bound to a weekend, format or qualifying key.

## 3. Approaches

- **A. A continued weekend** (revision 1): replaced; it binds to "the next weekend", a warm-up lobby can take it, and it cannot express "none".
- **B. Prepared plans keyed by the qualifying, committed at the race's first frame** (revision 2): replaced; it couples the plan to a basis choice made at race load (an override of the preselection loses the plans) and needs a second picker for the round.
- **C. One picker, saved plans as standalone things (picked, user 2026-11-18).** The basis and the plan are chosen together, by decisions in a session (the same pattern as the calibration choice, C62a), or as the round's input between sessions.

### 4.2 The basis and the picker

- **Domain types (supervisor, C78):** `SessionRef = { sessionUid; weekendId }`; `SessionBasis = { practice: SessionRef[]; qualifying: SessionRef | null }` (an empty list and `null` are "none"); the decision kind `basisChosen { basis: SessionBasis }`; the sidecar `{ start: SessionBasis }` at `<recording>.basis.json`.
- **The team's last choice** (bridge user state, C70 pattern, `PITWALL_DATA_DIR/basis/<team>.json`): per track the last basis and the last saved plan chosen or saved, with the time of the choice. Written by the picker, between sessions or in a session.
- **Preselection** (`apps/bridge/src/pipeline/session-basis.ts`, new): at the first Participants (`SessionProcessor.#contextFor`, `session-processor.ts:339`), per the C62 rule, per kind. Tonight: the morning's choice (2026-11-17 practice and qualifying) is preselected for the qualifying; a short practice in the new lobby, run after the morning's choice, takes the practice kind (the user overrides it in the picker if the plan was made on the long practice). Written to the sidecar at that moment.
- **Loading:** `SessionStorage.weekendContext` loads the basis's sessions of the feed's player seat (a `WeekendRepository` method `contextOf(refs, player)`). Today a stale session (an older rules version) is left out of the context and rebuilt in the background (`session-storage.ts:147`); after T1/T2 every session outside the Texas weekend is stale, so a picked one would silently be missing, and a later replay (rebuilt by then) would differ from the live run (C38). Therefore:
  - **the session-start basis** is loaded as today, but its stale sessions are rebuilt **before** the first frame is applied (the session start waits; progress shown in the top bar), never left out;
  - **a basis chosen in a session** is handed to the engine as a `basisChosen` decision only once every named session is loaded and current; until then the picker shows "loading…" and nothing is recorded;
  - **the picker marks stale candidates** and the bridge starts rebuilding them as soon as the picker lists them (one at a time, C61's job, memory: one reprocess at a time), so a choice is usually ready at once.
  The TEMP alias hack is deleted.
- **Changing in a session:** a uiAction `basis.choose { feedId, basis }` is committed as a `basisChosen` decision on each of the team's feeds (C1a, C76), after loading (above), each with its own current frame. **A feed that joins later** (the teammate's game connects after the choice) is handed the team's newest `basisChosen` decision at its first frame with its team known, next to the newest plan decisions it already gets (`FeedPipeline.#updateTeam`). `view.weekend` follows the newest one (like `view.priors`, C62a). The readers of `view.weekend` (`weekend-pace`, which already reads `decisions`; `shared/grid.ts`; `forecast-score/job.ts`; `track-wetness/dry-reference.ts`) declare `decisions` in their reads.
- **The picker** (one shell command "Basis and plan…", C72; the top bar shows "Practice 17 Nov · Qualifying 17 Nov · Plan: Texas 18 Nov 09:40", or "none" per part): three parts, practice (multi-select), qualifying (one), plan (one; only for a race or sprint, or between sessions); candidates are the stored sessions on the track (newest first, with dates) and the team's saved plans on the track; "none" for each. Between sessions the choice sets the round's input; in a session it commits decisions.
- **Topic:** `basis.active` per feed (C54): the basis, its session-start value, per session its date, kind and track, the chosen plan's name, and the team's last choice when no session runs.
- **CLI:** `pitwall basis [--feed id]` (show), `pitwall basis choose --practice <ref…|none> --qualifying <ref|none> [--plan <name|none>]`.
- **Replay and reprocess** read the sidecar and the decisions (C38).

### 4.3 The planning round and saved plans

- **The round** opens on a uiAction `plans.round.open { feedId }` whenever no session runs on the feed, on the picker's basis: the pipeline replays the qualifying's recording to its end (a new `FeedPipeline.restoreEndedNow(row)`: the startup restore's path, usable after start while between sessions, with packets that arrive meanwhile buffered as the restart restore does), with the basis's practice sessions as its weekend context. At a qualifying's live end the picker's basis becomes that weekend's practice and the qualifying (the round opens there as today).
- **Sets option** `raceSets: 'carried' | 'fresh'` per round, passed to `planningInputFrom` → `buildGridStart` (§4.4 item 3): `carried` (the qualifying's sets plus the race-reserved ones) by default when the round opens at the qualifying's live end, `fresh` (`freshRaceSets`) when it opens later on a stored qualifying (a new lobby gives a full allocation, user 2026-11-18); the board shows and switches it.
- **Saved plans** (`apps/bridge/src/planning/store.ts`, reworked): `SavedPlan = { id; name; team; trackId; savedAt; computedOn: { basis; laps; raceSets }; cars: PreparedCar[] }` (per race number its slots and active slot, as today), one file per plan in `plans/<team>/<id>.json`. uiActions `plans.save { feedId, name?, replaces? }` (from the open round, or from the in-session board), `plans.rename`, `plans.delete`. The existing `plans/548397152/grandPrix/0.json` and the `RaceKey` type are deleted (tonight's prepared plans are not needed).
- **Choosing a plan in a race or sprint:** the preselected plan (the team's last chosen or saved plan on this track) is committed at the first frame with the weekend phase decided and the team known, as `planSaved` per slot and `planCommitted` for the active slot, per car by race number (`#raceStart`, reworked); choosing another plan later commits its slots and clears the slots it lacks (`planRemoved`); choosing none clears none of the decisions already made (C24) and commits nothing. A plan whose `computedOn.laps` differs from the race's laps, or that uses a set the race does not have, is marked on the board (`strategy` judges feasibility, C43), never adjusted.
- **Plan none:** the in-session plan board works as today on `strategy.plans` (the race's own view, real grid, real sets); its slots are plan decisions, and `plans.save` saves them as a saved plan.
- **What is deleted:** `RaceKey`, the race-key matching (`sameKey`), the prepared plans' race-key commit, the `plans.prepared` topic's weekend and format fields (the topic becomes the round's state plus the team's saved plans on the track).

### 4.4 Planner fixes (no contract change; report §1–§4, 6g)

Each item: owner module, what changes, the report's tests. Every recorder or sub-machine change changes the rules version (C61, C90).

1. **Laps recorder: a lap completes only at a crossing of the line** (report §1; `engine/src/recorders/laps.ts`, `lap-boundary.ts`). A rise of the lap number without a crossing (lap distance falls by more than half the track length, or from below 0 to 0 or more) appends no lap and marks the lap number not completed, so Session History confirms nothing for it either. Segment tests on both qualifying recordings around 1115–1121 s.
2. **`car-activity-timed`: the in-lap status at the line is a pending confirmation** (report §2; `situations/car-activity-timed/timed.ts`, C18). Declared transitions unchanged; the transition table rows split (status mid-lap / on the tick before the line); segment tests DR 214–219 s (car 18), ASY 443–448 s (car 9); `pitwall scan-situations` over every recording in the report (C85).
3. **Race sets in the race's starting point** (report F3a; `analysis/src/shared/forecast-field`, the one builder, C50): on a grid start the sets are those `available` now plus those with `recommendedSession` equal to the race's session type. With the round's option `raceSets: 'fresh'` (§4.3) the builder plans with a fresh race allocation instead of the qualifying's sets: per car the dry compounds' counts of a race-only weekend's allocation (`freshRaceSets` in `forecast-field/config.ts`, C47, its defaults measured from the first TyreSets of the aborted race of 2026-11-18 00:37, recordings `2026-11-18_00-37-17_6ca2663a73e7b608_car18/19.pwrec.gz`). In a race the builder always reads the race's real sets. The fitted-set question is settled by the builder: the race's free start may use any set the race has (used ones included) and the DP's free start keeps taking a new set unless only used ones exist; marked as the builder's call in the plan.
4. **The rain filter is never bypassed; one owner** (report F3b, F3c): `bestPlanOf` and `candidatesOf` apply `allowed` to every fallback; with nothing allowed the car's entry is infeasible (C3). `rainCanCome`/`allowedOf` move to one shared pure function (or calculator) read by `strategy`, `pit-window` and the Box call (C43).
5. **One set-feasibility rule and the planning cap** (report F4b, F4c): one shared pure function "the sets a policy uses from a starting point" for the DP and `followable` (it also marks a chosen saved plan that uses a set the race lacks, §4.3); `partFinalCall` takes the cap from the chosen MC config (6,000 in the planning round, R34).
6. **Degradation α offsets as paired differences** (report §5 fix 1; `degradation/priors.ts`, its entry `version` changes, the priors store recomputes, C62a): per (session, car) the compound's α minus that car's medium α in the same session, then pooled; sources without a pair give ν but no offset. As a data step (no code): remove the 2026-11-04 race stints from `tx-1104` (report §5c).
7. **A declared pace stand-in** (report 6g; in `weekend-pace`, the owner of pre-race pace, C43): a car with no long run and no valid qualifying lap takes the field's median qualifying pace plus a config offset (`noPaceStandInOffsetMs`, `config.ts`, C47), marked `standIn: true` in the output; the grid start reads it like any pace. Without any qualifying evidence in the field (a basis without a qualifying) there is no stand-in: every car without its own evidence stays `unknown('insufficientData')` (G5). Tonight's TEMP HACK in `forecast-field` is deleted.
8. **The planning-round save on the teammate's board** (report 6a): the two silent-ok `invoke` defaults (`kit/commands.ts`, `Panel.svelte`) answer `notSupported`; the teammate board's panel gets the registry's `invoke`. (C72, C73.)

`minLongRunLaps` stays 8; to be checked in §6.

## 6. Readiness check (before the race)

A script in the research repo (`live-test-2026-11-17/readiness.mjs`, read-only on storage and the bridge's topics) and a short checklist entry. After the rebuild, with the bridge running, the picker set to the 2026-11-17 practice and qualifying, and the round opened (`plans.round.open`, sets `fresh`):

1. **Grid:** both feeds give the same grid, equal to the full-lap order (DR P1 1:35.365, Verstappen P2, … ASY P14 1:37.643, Hadjar P20).
2. **Pace:** every grid car has a `weekend-pace` value or a marked stand-in; no `insufficientData`.
3. **In-laps:** no AI flying lap of the qualifying is an in-lap on either feed.
4. **Sets:** the round plans with `freshRaceSets`; at the race's load, the real TyreSets of both our cars match it (else the chosen plan is marked and re-checked before lights out).
5. **Candidates:** both our cars get several candidates per stop count, none with inters (rain 1 %); DR's DP list contains a medium-medium-soft 2-stop.
6. **Calibration:** `tx-1104` without the 11-04 race and with paired offsets: the hard offset clearly slower than medium (DR's sources alone gave hard +1570 ms and soft +167 ms, report §5a); the values shown to the user before the race.
7. **Saved plan:** a plan saved from the round survives a bridge restart and is listed in the picker.
8. **Dry run:** a replay of a short race recording with a basis sidecar naming the 2026-11-17 practice and qualifying, and a decisions sidecar choosing the saved plan, shows `view.weekend` with those sessions and the plan's slots as plan decisions; with basis none, `weekend-pace` is `insufficientData` and nothing throws; a rewind past a basis choice falls back visibly.
9. **Loading and joining:** a choice naming a stale session is recorded only after its rebuild (the replay of that run shows the same `view.weekend` at the decision's frame); a second feed started after a choice on the first gets the same basis and plan decisions at its first frame with the team known.
10. **Tonight's preselection:** at the race's first Participants the top bar shows the morning's choice (qualifying 17 Nov, plan saved this morning; practice per §4.2); the user confirms or changes it before lights out.

A failing point is reported to the user with its effect on the race before the race starts.
