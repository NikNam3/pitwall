## 4. 4.3b1 part 0: the forecast repair (U9)

### 4.1 Why

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spa (`raceSpaSc`) | 0.77 / 0.62 | 1.71 / 1.35 | 2.31 / 2.27 | 4.1 s |
| Texas | 0.97 / 0.95 | 2.37 / 1.92 | 3.30 / 1.99 | 5.7 s |
| China | 0.83 / 0.59 | 1.48 / 1.31 | — | 1.9 s |
| Singapore | 0.26 / 0.12 | 0.88 / 0.29 | 1.64 / 0.86 | 7.3 s |

## 5. `race-hazard` and the track files (U4)

### 5.1 Track file

`TrackData.defaults` gains (domain, C78):

```ts
type TrackHazard = {
  /** Real races behind the numbers. */
  races: number;
  /** The chance an SC or a VSC starts on lap 1. */
  lapOne: { sc: number; vsc: number };
  /** The chance per lap that one starts, from lap 2, by race-distance fraction; ascending `upTo`, the last 1. */
  byFraction: { upTo: number; sc: number; vsc: number }[];
  /** The chance of a neutralisation lasting 1, 2, 3 … laps (index 0 = 1 lap); each sums to 1. */
  durationLaps: { sc: number[]; vsc: number[] };
};
// in defaults:
hazard: Known<TrackDefault<TrackHazard>>;
```

- **Made by `pitwall-trackdata`** (outside pitwall, C62b), before the 4.3b1 run: lap-level SC and VSC events 2015–2025 from the Kaggle "Formula 1 Race Events" dataset (Aguad's source; the user downloads it once, Kaggle needs a login), checked against OpenF1's race-control messages 2023–2025; each track smoothed towards the pooled shape over all tracks (lap 1 high, the rest flat; TUM's bins); the per-race rate checked against Aguad's Appendix A (`docs/research/14-aguad-appendix-a.csv`). The source names the races counted; Miami, Las Vegas and Losail lean on the pooled shape and say so.
- **Shorter races:** the per-lap chance is the same in a race at 25 % or 50 % distance, so a short race has fewer SCs. An assumption, stated in the source string.
- **Validation:** the track-file test (C62b) checks every committed file: chances in [0, 1], `byFraction` ascending to 1, each duration distribution sums to 1 within 1e-9.

### 5.2 `race-hazard` (calculator, quick)

- **Reads:** `view.track`, the lobby SC setting (live state, `GameSettings.safetyCar`), `spans:session-phase-race`, the leader's lap, `totalLaps`.
- **Cadence:** on change only (C41): a new leader lap, a `session-phase-race` span opened or closed or a neutralised stage added, the session settings.
- **Output, per remaining lap:** the start chance of an SC and of a VSC, scaled by the lobby setting; the duration distributions. For an open `neutralised` span: the distribution of its remaining laps, given the laps it has run (the duration distribution conditioned), and `returning` means it ends this lap.
- **No start** while a neutralisation is out and on the lap after it ends.
- **Lobby setting:** Off 0, Standard 1; Reduced and Increased config factors (`reducedFactor` 0.5, `increasedFactor` 1.5, pitwall estimates).
- **`Known`:** `unknown('notApplicable')` without a track file or its hazard and outside the race phase (C14); `insufficientData` before `totalLaps` is known (C3).
- **Behaviour table** over `session-phase-race` (C44): `racing` → the table's chances; `neutralised` → the remaining duration; `grid`, `formation`, `startLights` → the chances from lap 1; `suspended`, `finished`, `postSession` → `notApplicable`.

## 6. `strategy-dp` (simulator, per car)

Stops and compounds only (research 11 §4); ERS and fuel stay per-lap targets.

**Input** (plain, cloneable, from the `strategy` job's `prepare` through `forecast-field`): the car's lap-model parameters; the wear rate per compound, or age where wear is hidden; the sets left per compound (new sets only, K5); the compound rule (a second dry compound is owed: `compound-rule` `required` and fewer than two dry compounds used, the shared helper of F9); the pit loss per condition with the in-lap/out-lap split (F2); `race-hazard`'s per-lap chances and durations; the plain forecast's wetness path; a per-lap traffic cost for a rejoin on that lap, from the plain forecast.

**State:** lap; compound and tyre age in laps (wear converted to age through the car's wear rate where it is known); second dry compound used; sets left per compound; neutralisation (none, SC, VSC) with its laps left.

**Cost:** the expected remaining race time over the SC/VSC chances of each lap (Carrasco Heine & Thraves): a stop under an SC or VSC costs that condition's loss (about 0.5 × green where unmeasured); neutralised laps are slower and use less tyre and fuel (lap model). In a wet race inters and wets join the compounds, and the wetness path sets their lap time.

**Output:**
- the best base plan per stop count (0 to 3) and compound sequence, with stop laps; the "box this lap" and "stay out" variants of the best;
- the trigger thresholds (O9), from the table:
  - **SC/VSC:** per remaining stop, the lap range where boxing under this neutralisation now beats staying out past it (S25);
  - **damage:** the time loss per lap above which boxing now beats carrying it on;
  - **degradation off:** the change of wear rate at which the best sequence changes, by bisection over a few re-solves inside the budget;
  - **rain:** the wetness at which a switch of tyre type beats staying out, pit loss included;
- the remaining-race table's values, for the calls (`sc-call` shows the SC range from it, §9; C43).

The traffic term is approximate (research 13 §10): the job adds stop-lap neighbours (§8.1), the Monte Carlo chooses.

**Tests:** equal to brute force on small races (≤ 10 laps, ≤ 2 stops); with no SC chance and no wear, an extra stop never shortens the expected time; a zero SC chance gives the deterministic DP; the compound rule always holds; a seeded run is bit-identical. **Budget:** ≤ 300 ms per car, up to 3 stops, long-race builder at lap 1 (spike: 190 ms).

## 7. `race-monte-carlo` (simulator)

**Input:** the starting point (`forecast-field`), the candidate policies per car, a committed policy where one exists, `race-hazard`'s tables, the job's generator.

### 7.1 Scenarios

Every draw from a stream keyed by (scenario, car, lap, purpose), derived from the injected generator by hashing (C2, S25):

- neutralisations (`race-hazard`);
- weather: a rain timeline from the forecast's rain chance per sample, wider when the forecast is approximate (S12); the wetness state evolves from it; the lap model's wetness term turns it into lap time;
- pace uncertainty: every car's true pace level and degradation slope around its estimates, σ from their standard errors (S25, research 14 proposal 14);
- every car's scatter and mistakes (`driver-noise`; profiles for our seats) and retirements at the field rate;
- rivals' stop laps and compounds (`rival-stops`, with F3).

### 7.2 Rivals' reaction rules (O8)

- **Under an SC**, a rival that still owes a stop boxes with chance `q` once its tyres are at least `aMin` laps old. Spa (`raceSpaSc`, leader lap 20): 16 of the 18 cars that owed a stop with tyres 6–10 laps old boxed; cars 8 (7 laps) and 2 (8 laps) stayed out; car 17 (2 laps) stayed out. `q` and `aMin` are config, fitted on the SC cases of Spa and Texas in a task with its data counts, marked thin.
- **Under a VSC** a rival never boxes (research 12 §6); checked again on Mexico's VSC in the same task.
- **In the wet**, a rival switches type at the lap its type falls behind in the scenario's crossover, after `reactionLaps` (config), and at once under an SC.
- **No cover rule** (research 12: the AI does not cover undercuts). Ablation (research 14 proposal 15): a test case with a cover rule switched on reports whether the ranking changes; the result goes into the run report and §12.3.

Our cars follow their policies; their triggers fire inside the scenario; a committed policy is followed exactly (S14); both our cars boxing on the same lap pay the double-stack wait (4.3a).

### 7.3 The search

1. A first batch of `n0` scenarios (30–50) for every candidate, common random numbers.
2. Candidates with identical outcomes on every scenario so far are merged.
3. Further scenarios go to the policies still close to the leader; a policy is dropped only when worse than the leader by `zDrop` (2.8) standard errors of the paired difference.
4. Stop when the leader is clear or at the cap. The cap is a count of scenario-policies (`maxScenarioPolicies`, config, 5,000: research 14 §2 item 3), sized so the run fits 5 s single thread (S26) and proved by the budget test; no unit reads a clock (C2), and a replay gives the live result exactly.
5. The shown top few (`rescoreTop` 5) are scored again together on fresh common scenarios, inside the cap.

**Ranking** (S5 as revised by S25; research 14 proposal 1): by E[position]; a policy ties with the leader when its paired difference is below max(`tieBand` 0.05, `zTie` 2 × SE); tied policies by E[points] with the same test against their leader; then by mean race time. A near-tie unresolved at the cap is flagged.

### 7.4 Output per policy

The finishing-position distribution P(P1) … P(P20) (U6: the graphs); E[position], E[points] (the league points table in config), mean race time, position quantiles; the chance to gain a place, measured at the finish (research 14 B §7); per trigger how often it fired and what it was worth; its source (`dp`, `neighbour`, `rule`, `committed`), its paired difference and SE against the leader, its tie flag; the run's scenario count.

**Tests:** SC counts and start laps match `race-hazard`'s table within 1/√N; two cars with known spreads give their analytic position chances; the error falls as 1/√N; a committed policy is followed in every scenario; one more stop of one candidate leaves every other draw unchanged; the plain forecast is one mode of the distribution; good selection: on the test race at the largest candidate count, the winner at the cap lies within 0.05 places of a 50,000-scenario reference; a split-or-stack case (both our cars, one lap apart against the same lap) decides whether the mistake process and the double stack stay (S22, S27).

**The good-selection reference** (about 1.5 M scenario-policies, tens of minutes) is never computed in a test run. A script (`pnpm gen:strategy-reference`) computes it once, offline, after `strategy` exists: the test race's candidates from `strategy`'s candidate builder, each scored on all 50,000 common scenarios (a full evaluation, no search, nothing dropped). It commits `test-support/fixtures/strategy-reference.json` (supervisor, C78) with the candidate list, every candidate's E[position] and a source hash over `packages/analysis/src/**` (tests excluded) and `test-support/race-generator/**`. The test (in `strategy.budget.test.ts`) feeds the stored candidates to the capped search, compares its winner with the reference and fails with "regenerate the reference" when the hash differs. `maxScenarioPolicies` is set from the measured cost per scenario before the reference is made; if the count good selection needs does not fit 5 s, that is a question for the user, not a lower bar.

## 8. The `strategy` job, the plan decision, the CLI

### 8.1 `strategy` (analysis with a job, C51)

One job per feed (C1a), both our cars in one run (S4, S14).

**Feeds (U12, §8.5).** A feed's job sees only its own view (C39), but a plan committed for either car reaches every feed of the team (C76 as changed, §2.4): each feed's job plans both cars with the teammate's committed plan as certain input. Each car's rows are shown from that car's own feed, which has the full data on it (§10); the other feed's rows for it are its view of the teammate and are not shown.

- **`prepare(view)`:** `forecast-field`'s starting point, `race-hazard`'s tables, the newest plan decision per car from the projection; cloneable. ≤ 20 ms.
- **`run(input, rng)`** in a worker:
  1. candidates per car: the DP's base plans and box-now and stay-out variants; their stop-lap neighbours ±1–3 laps; the rule candidates mirror ahead, mirror behind, cover, extend (S21); the committed policy as one marked row (U3). Each with the DP's trigger thresholds from the closed list (high-level §3.4);
  2. our two cars: a committed policy is certain input for the teammate; without one on either car, round 1 plans each against the other modelled like a rival, round 2 against the other's round-1 best; each car's alternatives are always ranked (U3); no state between runs (C31, C32);
  3. `race-monte-carlo` over every candidate; the cap (`maxScenarioPolicies`, sized to **5 s single thread**, S26, §7.3) covers both cars.
- **Seed:** the job runner's existing `jobSeed` (`apps/bridge/src/jobs/job-runner.ts`: job id, lap, frame id); the session uid is not in the view and is not needed. With the scenario-count cap (§7.3), a replay of a frame gives the live run's result exactly and `pitwall strategy` matches the live job for the frames the live job ran on.
- **Cadence:** each line crossing of either of our cars; events: any stop, an SC/VSC start or end, a plan decision. The newest run wins; a rewind cancels and re-triggers (C30).
- **Topic** `strategy.plans` (state, `Computed<StrategyOutput>`): per our car the ranked rows (§7.4) and the run's metadata (scenarios, cap hit, candidate count). Excluded from the determinism hash (C38, C51).
- **Reads:** `decisions`, the estimation calculators, `race-hazard`. Behaviour tables over `session-phase-race`, `car-activity-race`, `car-driver`, `compound-rule` (C44). Outside the race phase `notApplicable` (C14).

### 8.2 The plan decision (C76; domain, C78)

- `planCommitted { carId, policy }`: the base plan (stops: lap and compound) and its triggers with their thresholds as at the commit; followed exactly until replaced or cleared (S14, U3).
- `planCleared { carId }`.
- **Why the policy is stored whole (C23, C76):** the policy, thresholds included, is what the user chose, not a unit's output: the decision records the choice as the user saw it. A reference to a job result could not be replayed (job results are outside the determinism hash, C38, C51), so the decision carries the plan itself. Analyses read it from the projection; nothing recomputes it.
- uiActions `plan.commit`, `plan.clear`; commands `plan.commit`, `plan.clear` (C72); the bridge validates their shape and range only (C73: `carId` a car slot, stop laps ascending and within the race's laps, known compounds); whether a plan can still be followed is the `strategy` job's judgement (C43): a committed policy that cannot (a stop lap passed, no set left) is flagged `infeasible` with its reason and scored as far as it can be followed (a passed stop boxes this lap; a compound without a set left keeps the current tyres);
- the bridge writes them to the decisions sidecar of every feed of the team and hands them to each feed's engine at its current frame (C76 as changed, §2.4); the decision recorder appends them (C10); a flashback voids them (C27a).
- 4.3b1 builds the kind, the uiActions, the commands and the bridge's handling; the strategy table's buttons come in 4.3b2 (§10).

### 8.3 `pitwall strategy <recording> --at-lap n [--car id] [--json <file>]`

The `pitwall score` mechanism (4.3a §7.2): reprocess to the frame of our car's crossing of lap n, need `strategy.plans`, wait for the job's result, print per car the ranked rows (source, stops, triggers, E[pos], E[points], mean time, the position distribution as a compact line, the tie flag) and the run's metadata; `--json` writes it. No result: an empty report, exit 0 (R50).

### 8.4 `forecast-score`: CRPS

Per horizon, the CRPS of the Monte Carlo's position distribution against what happened (for whole places, the ranked probability score: the sum over places of the squared difference of the cumulative distributions); AI and human apart, like its other tables.

- **One run per predict lap, not per case:** a scenario simulates the whole field, so one run of `crpsScenarios` (config, 200) scenarios over the plain plans, from the predict lap to the largest horizon, gives every car's distribution at every horizon. It is `race-monte-carlo`'s second entry, `fieldPositions` (every car's position distribution at given laps, a fixed scenario count, no search), not the policy search, which reports our cars' finishing positions only.
- **No neutralisations drawn:** the scored cases hold no SC or VSC lap (F1), so the run draws none (the hazard set to zero); otherwise the spread of SC scenarios would be scored against SC-free outcomes.
- **Monte Carlo settings** (the rivals' rule, the points table) are `strategy`'s config, read through `view.config` (as `race-moment` reads `battle`'s `drsRangeMs`); the 4.3b2 calls read them the same way.
- **Predict laps** every `crpsEveryLaps` (config, 5) laps, from lap 2; SC/VSC cases left out as in F1.
- **Budget:** the CRPS share ≤ 1.0 s at lap 70 of the long-race builder (14 predict laps × 200 scenarios × 10 laps at about 0.02 ms per field lap, from 4.3a's 0.6 ms per 35 laps), so the job ≤ 1.5 s with 4.3a's 0.5 s. A measurement over the budget lowers `crpsScenarios` or raises `crpsEveryLaps`, never the budget.

### 8.5 One desktop, both drivers (U12)

- **Several feeds in one bridge** (C1a, C58, ADR 0009): `pitwall live` opens one UDP source and one feed pipeline per port in `PITWALL_UDP_PORT` (`20777,20778`); one hub, one WebSocket; `pnpm desktop` passes the variable through. Storage is already keyed by the feed's player car (C60), so two feeds of one session are stored side by side.
- **The team's feeds** (C1a as changed): feeds with the same session uid whose player cars have the same team (Participants). The bridge keeps this pairing as bookkeeping of its feeds, no judgement (C43); a feed whose team is not known yet is in no team. `pitwall live` with one port is a team of one: nothing changes.
- **Plan routing** (C76 as changed): `plan.commit` and `plan.clear` go to every feed of the team, each at its engine's current frame, into each feed's decisions sidecar; a feed that joins later gets each car's newest plan at its first frame with its team known. Every other decision (`calibrationChosen`) stays with its feed.
- **Flashbacks:** online sessions have none, so the team's logs cannot diverge through one; offline there is one feed.
- **Out:** the teammate feed's values in a unit's view (ADR 0009's team view): a later contract change, if a feed's own view of the teammate proves too thin.

## 9. The calls (4.3b2, U6)

Each an analysis with a small Monte Carlo job (C51), its starting point from `forecast-field`, run through `race-forecast`, `strategy-dp` and `race-monte-carlo` with the keyed streams of §7.1. Each job's cap is a scenario-policy count, as `strategy`'s (§7.3): sized to its budget (§11.2), proved by its budget test; a measurement over the budget lowers the count, never raises the budget. Seeds and feeds as `strategy`'s (§8.1). Run where a topic is subscribed (C49). Cadence: each line crossing of our car; any stop; an SC/VSC start or stage; a plan decision. The newest run wins.

**Topics per car.** Each call's topic takes `carId` (the link group's primary car, as every per-car display): the job's output is keyed by our cars and the binding selects the entry, as `bindState` with `selectCar` does (`bindJob` gains the same `select`; a bridge change before the calls). The strategy table reads `strategy.car` the same way, the per-car topic beside `strategy.plans`, which `pitwall strategy` reads whole.

### 9.1 `undercut` → `undercut.car`

- **Rivals:** the car directly ahead and the car directly behind within `undercutRangeMs` (config, 3,000 ms: `forecast-score`'s `closeGapMs`, research 12 §8, so the call and its scoreboard cases are the same cases), and the next one each way when within range.
- **Per rival and N = 1–3:** we box this lap and the rival N laps later on its predicted next compound; for a car behind, the mirror (it boxes now, we N later).
- **Per case:** the expected gap change once both have stopped (positive = good for us), the chance we are ahead then, the gain distribution in bins (0.5 s from −4 to +4 s), the rejoin position; per rival its place and current gap (the card's dotted line: bins right of it end ahead).
- **Horizon:** until both have stopped, plus one lap. **Budget** ≤ 300 ms per car. **Cap:** `maxScenarioPolicies` 2,400 (up to 4 rivals × 3 N × 2 policies, 100 common scenarios each; horizons of a few laps).

### 9.2 `pit-sweep` → `pitSweep.car`

- **Params:** our car and `compound: 'auto' | Compound`; `auto` (the default) is `pit-window`'s next compound, resolved by `pit-sweep` itself from `pit-window`'s output (C43), never by the panel or the display (C65).
- **Per stop lap L** from now to the end of the window: the policy "box at L onto the compound, then the DP's best continuation", to the flag.
- **Per row:** rejoin position, traffic (cars within `trafficGapMs` in the first `trafficLaps` laps; config 1,000 ms, the DRS line that decides an undercut, Aguad fn. 15; 3 laps, where traffic losses are settled, research 13 §10.2, Tulabandhula & Rudin), E[finish position], the position distribution, the chance to gain a place, mean time, whether the stint exceeds the compound's life (`tyre-life`).
- **The pit window:** the rows within the tie test of the best (research 14 proposal 11).
- **Every compound swept:** a unit sees no topic params (C39), so the job sweeps every compound the car has a new set of (`pit-window`'s sets), each within its own cap, and names the one `auto` resolves to; the topic's select picks the panel's compound. The budget below is per compound; the job's time is their sum.
- **Budget** ≤ 1.5 s per car and compound. **Cap:** `maxScenarioPolicies` 1,000 (to the flag; about 1.5 ms per scenario at lap 1).

### 9.3 `sc-call` → `scCall.car` (from quick to a job)

- **Per condition SC and VSC, "if one came now":** box under it now against staying out, the neutralisation forced at the current lap; the E[position] difference, the chance to gain, the distribution, the rejoin position (from `rejoin`, C43), the loss and its source, the DP's SC/VSC trigger range for the next stop (§6, C43).
- **With an SC or VSC out:** the same for the real condition with its remaining duration (`race-hazard`); the other condition's row is `notApplicable` (no second neutralisation starts while one is out; mockup Q5 B).
- **Per row:** the ΔE[pos] of boxing against staying out (places, positive = good), P(gain) (boxing finishes ahead), the places-gained distribution (−4 … +4), the rejoin position, the loss with its source, the trigger range. The 4.2 row's `gainMs` goes.
- **Budget** ≤ 500 ms per car. **Cap:** `maxScenarioPolicies` 320 (SC and VSC × box and stay out, 80 common scenarios each, to the flag).

### 9.4 Agreement (C43, D8)

For one seed: `pit-sweep`'s row at the stop lap of `strategy`'s best policy has that policy's E[position] within their two MC errors; `undercut`'s box-now case agrees with `strategy`'s box-now variant over the same horizon. A test each.

**Compute:** per feed, `strategy` and three small jobs in their own workers: with two feeds about eight busy workers in a burst, within the machine's 22 cores. The job runtime keeps one run per job at a time (4.3a).
