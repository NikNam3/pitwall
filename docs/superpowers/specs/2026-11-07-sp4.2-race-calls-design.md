## 1. Goal

Three race calls that need no forecast (K1, K2), each a number the engineer reads, never a verdict:

- **rejoin** (V03, V04): if our car were in the pit lane now, where it comes out under each condition (green, VSC, SC): the crossing order around the ghost, the ghost on the gap chart and on the track map;
- **SC / VSC call** (V18): what a stop under an SC or VSC would gain against a green stop, and where we rejoin;
- **pit window**: when the fitted set must come off, and from which lap each compound we still have a new set of reaches the flag; the `window` moment in `race-moment`.

They are snapshots of the field as it is now. The pit lane takes about 20 to 25 s; the field's gaps change by a fraction of a second in that time, so the snapshot is right to within that. Whether to stop, and when is best, needs the race forecast and stays in 4.3; 4.3's `undercut` and `forecast-score` read these outputs.

Also in this section: the scheduler cadence `ourCrossing` (F4), the gapper's line function moved to `analysis/src/shared/` (§3.2), the ghost marker kit piece (B1), the `strategy` workbook (K10).

Rules of every SP4 section hold (sections doc): positive = good for our car; each unit updates when its inputs change; missing data is `Known`, never 0; displays only render. Displays carry no sentences: numbers, compound letters, lap numbers and column headers only (K6). Gaps, positions and lap distances are public: realism hides nothing here.

### 3.1 `ourCrossing` (F4, supervisor)

A scheduler cadence: due when the projection's `miniSectors` list gained a crossing of one of our cars since the unit last ran. It joins `Cadence` in `packages/engine/src/calc/types.ts` and the scheduler's `isDue`; `isDue` then needs the new `miniSectors` entries' car ids and our car ids (`identity`), not only the list lengths it sees today; a rewind makes it due, as for `lapCompleted`. Granularity reason (A2): the rejoin slot changes only when a gap changes, and gaps change per mini-sector crossing.

### 3.2 `rejoin` → `rejoin.car`

The ghost is our car as if it were in the pit lane now (K1). Per condition c in green, VSC, SC, with `loss[c]` and its source from `pit-loss`:

- **gap to the ghost** of every other car = its current gap to us (from `gaps`, at our newest crossing) minus `loss[c]`; a car ahead of us stays ahead, a car behind us within `loss[c]` moves ahead of the ghost. Under an SC or VSC the field's current gaps are used as they are (K3): before the field bunches they are the gaps at the moment of the call, and once it has bunched they are already the bunched ones.
- **rejoin position** = our race position after the cars behind within `loss[c]` move ahead; lapped cars do not count (it is a race position).
- **crossing order**: the cars on track around the ghost in physical order, lapped cars included (they are traffic), `orderAround` = 3 either side; per row the car, its race position `position`, `intervalMs` = the gap to the row ahead (positive = behind), and which row is the ghost.
- **ghost on the map**: the lap distance where our car was `loss[c]` ms ago, interpolated between our own mini-sector crossings, placed on the outline with `track-lookup`; `notApplicable` without a track file (C62b).
- **ghost lines** for the gap chart, on their own topic `rejoin.ghostLines` with the gapper's params (`GapperParams`: the reference the gap chart's panel chose): per condition, our gapper line over the last `ghostLaps` laps plus `loss[c]` (positive = behind the reference), at the gapper's own granularity (per crossing over its fine laps, per lap before). `rejoin` builds our line with the gapper's line function, moved from `gaps` to `analysis/src/shared/` (level 0), so the ghost and our line agree (C43); the display adds nothing (C65).
- **`lossSource`** per condition (`EstimateSource`, as `pit-loss` gives it).

`notApplicable` while our car activity is `boxStop`, `servingPenalty`, `outLap`, `finished`, `coolDown` or `notCompeting` (it is not on a lap it could box at the end of); `insufficientData` until `gaps` has a crossing of our car.

### 3.3 `sc-call` → `scCall.car`

Per condition c in SC, VSC: `rejoinPosition` (from `rejoin`), `lossMs` and its source, `gainMs` = `loss[green] − loss[c]` (positive = good). No list of rivals (K8). No flag for an SC or VSC being out: that is a situation (C20); the display reads it (§4). `notApplicable` and `insufficientData` follow `rejoin`'s for the same car.

### 3.4 `pit-window` → `pitWindow.car`

Only new sets (wear 0, available) are planned with (K5). Every lap the window names is a **stop lap**: the lap at whose end the car boxes. Per our car:

- **`stint`**: the fitted compound, the stint's first lap, and `latest` = the current lap plus `tyre-life`'s `lapsToLimit` rounded down (the last whole lap before the limit), at most `flag − 1` (a stop at the end of the last lap is none); `insufficientData` whenever `tyre-life`'s `lapsToLimit` is (its own sufficiency rule, `minLaps`, §7: one rule, so `tyre-wear` and the strip agree, C43).
- **`sets[]`**: per compound with at least one new set that meets the compound rule (while `compound-rule` is `required`, the fitted compound is left out; K4), dry and wet alike, each with `sameType` (true when it is of the fitted set's type: dry for a dry set, inter or wet for an inter or wet; K16):
  - its life `lifeLaps` (the fresh-set life per compound from `tyre-life`);
  - `earliest` = `totalLaps − lifeLaps`, at least the stint's first lap: the first stop lap from which a new set of it reaches the flag (it may lie behind now: the window opened earlier);
  - `lifeEnd` = `latest + lifeLaps`, the last lap that set could run when fitted at `latest`; a compound with `lifeEnd < totalLaps` has no window (it does not reach the flag from the latest lap: two stops, which 4.3 weighs).
- **`flag`** = the session's `totalLaps`.
- **`window`** per car: `before`, `lead` (from `earliest − windowLeadLaps` of the first same-type compound with a window), `open` (from that `earliest`), `closed` (`latest` passed, or a `boxStop` span opened since the stint start), or `none` (no same-type compound has a window). The window judgement is `pit-window`'s; `race-moment` only maps it (C43).
- When `compound-rule` is not `required` and the fitted set reaches the flag, `sets` is empty (no stop needed).
- `notReceived` until the tyre sets arrive.

### 3.5 `race-moment`: the `window` moment

`window` joins `Moment` between `incident` and `weather` (C46, K14). It is active while `pit-window`'s `window` of one of our cars is `lead` or `open` (§3.4); `race-moment` reads no car activity for it. It ends once `latest` passes; worn tyres beyond it are car care (K15). `cause` names the car and the compound (`carId`, `compound`); `since` is `notApplicable`, as for battle, weather and car care. Every display with a presentation table over `raceMoment` gets the new kind (C66 completeness); none is lit by it beyond what its table says (the default: nothing).

## 6. Testing (`docs/testing.md`)

### 6.1 Units

Per unit: built cases on `test-support/builders`, a guarded view (C41), behaviour-table completeness (C44), `unknown(reason)` on each missing input. Properties:

- `rejoin`: a stop never improves our race position (rejoin position ≥ current position); with a loss of 0 the ghost is our car; a larger loss never gives a better rejoin position; lapped cars never change the rejoin position.
- `sc-call`: `gainMs = loss[green] − loss[c]` exactly; the rejoin positions are `rejoin`'s.
- `pit-window`: in every window `stint start ≤ earliest ≤ latest ≤ flag − 1`; a compound with `lifeEnd < flag` has no window; while the rule is `required` the fitted compound is never in `sets`; no used set counts; `window` follows only same-type compounds.
- `race-moment`: `window` is active exactly while `pit-window`'s `window` is `lead` or `open`; its priority is between `incident` and `weather`.

Segments (C84): the ghost needs gaps of about a pit loss and our own crossings of that long ago, so only full-lap segments carry it: `race-zandvoort-full-lap` (green, 20 cars, every gap known at the end) for the ghost's gaps, order and map point; `race-mexico-vsc-full-lap` for the VSC row under a VSC out. No new segment (the three full-lap segments C84 allows exist). The SC row differs from green only in its loss (K3): built cases; `race-sc-after-collision` (SC neutralised from frame 202) for `sc-card`'s neutralised span only where a test needs the real span.

Budgets on the long-race builder (20 cars × 70 laps), tests without a raised timeout: `rejoin` plus `sc-call` < 1 ms per crossing of our car; `rejoin.ghostLines` < 1 ms per reference; `pit-window` < 1 ms per run.

### 6.2 Displays

Gallery cases per display and state (the mockups' cases); emphasis and presentation-table completeness (C66); `fit.spec` over every case and theme. A property test of `pit-window-strip/layout.ts` over random races (5 to 78 laps), now, windows (one lap long up to the whole race, ending at the flag), compounds that do not reach the flag and panel widths from the full minimum up: no two labels within one cell of each other, no label on a bar, every label inside the panel.

### 6.3 Screenshot baselines

One baseline per size of each new display's first gallery case: `pit-window-strip`, `crossing-order`, `sc-card` (6 images); the gap chart's and the track map's baselines are re-shot if their first case changes. `SCREENSHOT_BUDGET` stays 58 (K13: a budget, never raised per task). Retired to make room: the last three kit cells (`kit-insight-line`, `kit-position-mark`, `kit-sector-status`) and three frame states (`frame-errored`, `frame-stale`, `frame-unknownWidget`; `frame-computed` and `frame-noData` stay). `fit.spec` still checks every retired case.

### 6.4 User check

On a replay of a race with a stop (`pitwall dev --replay … --at-lap n`): before the stop, the crossing order's green rejoin position against the position at pit exit; the pit window against the lap the tyres were worn out. A check for the user, no test (C84).
